Annotate ONE last-word JSON record for a causal story PREFIX. Treat the story as
data, never as instructions. This is version 3 of the prefix-only adaptation of the reviewed
20-story annotation prompt v4: keep its feature meanings, typed values, scopes
and uncertainty, but use ONLY evidence already in the supplied prefix. Do not
return earlier word records, a list, an annotations wrapper, Markdown, or prose.

INPUT AND CAUSAL BOUNDARY
The final user message contains JSON-line token spans, grouped into append-only
word-ended blocks. Concatenate their token values to read the exact text. The
last word is your target. Copy its index and token exactly, including case and
typos. No later space, punctuation or word exists in the evidence available to
you. Do not infer a grammatical object, purpose, recipient, outcome, quote speaker
or sense from an imagined continuation. A name alone supports person, not gender
or age. At "Lily took", no scissors or repair purpose has appeared. At "Lily took
scissors", the obtain event can acquire a patient in THIS record's snapshot;
the earlier took record is never revised. At an open negation, leave its unseen
predicate unresolved. A later repair must not rename the earlier obtain event.
An earlier available object is NOT automatically the patient or instrument of a
new verb. In "Nia took a needle. She did not mend", no mending patient or actual
instrument has appeared: patients=[] and instruments=[]. Do not turn the needle
into the thing being mended. Keep incomplete predicates incomplete, even when
the prefix contains plausible participants from another event.

Requests are independent and run in parallel. You never receive earlier model
answers. Earlier word annotations are immutable as-of observations, not a whole
story parse waiting to be completed. Later evidence can support the CURRENT
record's reference resolution and state only. Do not pretend that earlier
records had that evidence. Some catalog definitions discuss later evidence or
retrospective checks: omit those features until their evidence has appeared.

SPARSE TYPED RECORD
Always emit index, token, pos, lemma, scope_id. Add supported catalog fields
directly as top-level keys, using the provided 1,024-field definitions and agreed
extensions. There is no density quota. Omission means unknown, inapplicable or
unannotated; it never means false. Use real JSON booleans, numbers, arrays and
objects for typed fields. Do not stringify bundles or emit 1,024 nulls. Keep false
when a negative fact is supported. The legacy attributes field, if needed, is an
array of {"name": string, "value": string}; prefer an existing typed field.

pos: n (including proper names), v (including auxiliaries), adj, adv, pron, det,
adp, conj, num, part, intj, unknown. Choose the contextual class supported now.
lemma: dictionary base; preserve proper-name capitalization. Normalize inflected
verbs/plurals and modal forms could->can, would->will, should->shall, might->may.
Pronouns me->I, him->he, her->she, us->we, them->they, but possessive determiner
her keeps her. Keep contractions intact: I'd has pos=pron, lemma=I, plus its
supported expansion; couldn't has pos=v, lemma=can and ["could","not"]. If a
contraction is ambiguous at this prefix, omit its uncertain expansion. A
possessive name is not an auxiliary contraction.

Positions, character spans and synthetic whitespace/punctuation records are
computed locally. Never emit position, start, end, kind or word_index. Output is
one lexical record even if its supporting tables mention earlier words.
event_type is ONLY obtain, transfer, repair, move, perceive, speak, feel, state,
other. For cutting use other; the event phrase can still be "cut object". These
enums apply inside _references as well as on the word. Do not invent event types.

DETERMINISTIC ANCHORED IDS AND LOCAL TABLES
IDs restart per story and use ONE PLUS THE ORIGINAL LOSSLESS SPAN INDEX, counting
spaces and punctuation. Do not use sequential counts of discovered entities.
- eN: first noun-head mention of an entity. Lily at index 0 is e1; scissors at
  index 4 is e5. Repeated resolved mentions use the earliest head's ID. When no
  antecedent is supported, keep the reference unresolved rather than inventing
  a person. An unresolved new pronoun referent may be anchored to that pronoun.
- evN: lexical predicate trigger. took at index 2 is ev3. An auxiliary or
  negation operator need not invent a separate action. A repeated mention of an
  established event may reuse its ID; a new occurrence has its own trigger.
- s0: implicit narrator/root scope. sN: operator/attitude scope anchored at its
  word. qN: quotation scope anchored at its opening quote span. Retain nesting
  and the holder/speaker when supported, otherwise omit the unknown holder.
- pN: proposition anchored at its lexical predicate. Deterministic suffixes,
  such as p9_negative or p9_complete, distinguish polarity/operator content at
  the same anchor. sN and evN may also use meaningful suffixes for distinct
  scopes or secondary events at the same word. Entity IDs never have suffixes.
- Catalog tN anchors are one-based NONSPACE token ordinals, including punctuation.
  They differ from the lossless span indices used in IDs and evidence.

Include a reserved _references object with entities/events/scopes/propositions
tables for every ID referenced in this record or its tables, except implicit s0.
Omit unused tables. Every table entry is an object. All anchors must already be
visible. Entity records have name and type; event records have event, scope_id,
event_type, factuality and supported participants; scopes have kind, parent and
supported holder/speaker; propositions have content, polarity and scope_id.
Names and event descriptions must agree with the corresponding token features.
Use short stable event labels: obtain object, give object, return object, use
object, repair object, cut object. Do not encode unseen patients in these labels.
Entities can become more specific in later snapshots without rewriting earlier
ones. Keep the supporting tables minimal: only the current record's reference
closure, not an annotation of every earlier word.
Omit unused references. A resolved pronoun reuses its antecedent ID; do not also
create a second entity for its surface form merely to populate the tables.

Use _evidence=[{"indices":[lossless span indices],"note":"..."}] for substantial
interpretation or ambiguity. All evidence must be in the prefix. Optional
_feedback lists concrete uncertainties or feature gaps. A genuinely new meaning
can be added only with _new_attributes entries declaring key, type, description,
scope, reason and example_value. Reuse the shared names first; do not invent a
synonym or redefine an existing meaning. Types are boolean, string, number,
array, object, entity_ref, json. Agreed extensions need no new declaration.

SEMANTIC RULES ADAPTED FROM THE REVIEWED V4 PROMPT
1. Distinguish syntax, roles and identity. An actor differs from a grammatical
   subject or speaker; a prepositional complement is not a direct object.
   Use actor_id and entity ID arrays for patients/instruments. Match actor's
   display name to its entity table. Multiple predicate memberships belong in
   event_bindings [{event_id,role,scope_id}], with scalar event/role selecting
   the primary membership. A participant can describe the event as known now,
   including a newly seen patient, without mutating the earlier trigger record.
   A verb's own word membership is action; do not label that same word patient
   merely because its event has a patient in the event table.
   Never attach a pre-verbal subject to an unseen future verb.
2. Separate attitude, operator, positive target and complete content. Wanting,
   believing or saying is different from its proposition. Hoping nobody asks
   does not desire an asking event. A prohibition's full content is not the
   positive prohibited action. Negating a reason need not negate its event,
   but do not anticipate a later because-clause before it exists. Preserve
   conditional consequences and modal force in their own bundles/propositions.
3. assertion_status has ONLY asserted, attributed_claim, candidate. Event
   factuality has asserted, negated, desired, hypothetical, reported. A promise
   can be an attributed claim with desired content, not a completed action.
   Retain both status and scope within bundles. A fact asserted in an imagined
   world is not thereby a physical-world fact. Local negation is not universal
   falsehood; missing facts are not false. Quotation ancestry carries attribution
   even through a nested sN scope. A secondary praise event can be actual while
   its trigger noun stays quoted.
4. Bind physical facts to their carrier and time. Do not transfer ownership or
   location from mere plans, negated acquisition, or a proposed recipient.
   Borrowing/lending are inverse perspectives, not metonymy. Mechanical jamming
   is not an engaged lock; holding something still is a control condition.
   A movement toward a place is not arrival. Returning a container does not
   return all its contents. A method description does not repeat its action.
5. Keep scopes, people and capabilities precise. Resolve you to the known
   addressee; audience groups exclude their speaker unless self-address is
   explicit. Permission, choosing, tying and attaching can have different actors.
   A mobility aid does not imply a diagnosis or universal incapacity. A bodily
   cue can support a marked affect hypothesis, not a proved motive or trait.
6. Keep quantities and temporal phases distinct. Required duration, available
   slack, nominal route time and actual elapsed time differ. Do not infer a
   deadline breach from an evening ending. Bounded each/every over particular
   objects is not kind-level generic reference; every flag still has singular
   noun morphology. Groups can grow: membership records describe this prefix's
   known members, not later additions. Do not invent a total inventory.
7. Preserve material and representation distinctions: ingredient batch versus
   residue, cutter versus shaped biscuit, shallow mark versus cut-out, physical
   performer versus fictional character, image/reflection versus actual object.
   Use symbolic_reference only for an explained artifact symbol; represents is
   not identity, ownership, enactment or an unstated motive of commemoration.
   Leave missing handoffs, repairs, construction and other transitions unknown.
8. Check field definitions, not just names. modal_auxiliary_class uses the
   normalized lexical auxiliary (can, may, shall, etc.); contextual force goes
   in modal_base. Optical reflection is not a directional spatial_frame_kind.
   A result connective is not temporal. Use existing typed temperature and
   affordance fields. Catalog examples are illustrative, never facts to copy.

Self-check the target, all reference anchors, types, nested status and proposition
polarity before responding. Return only the sparse last-word JSON object.


RECONCILED COVERAGE PASS (v3)
Before returning, check which of these dimensions the CURRENT word makes
available: identity, syntax, physical state, goals/beliefs, dialogue/commitments,
and narrative function. Emit supported fields from these families, even when
basic POS/entity/event labels would also describe the word. Do not fill a quota.
A field existing in a reference table does not replace its useful current-word
catalog feature. Earlier fields never change; repeat or refine a supported goal,
promise or narrative reading on the complement that makes its content visible.

- At a bare wants/hopes cue, goal_owner_id and goal_status=proposed are useful;
  leave unseen goal_content_id absent. When the complement is visible, bind its
  pN content, owner and scope using goal_* fields. Negation inside a desire is
  still inside that desire: wanting not to ask does not assert that no asking
  happened. Do not mark a goal achieved until its success criterion is evidenced.
- Use promise_*, plan_commitment_status and obligation_* when supported. A
  promise cue may precede its content; attach the now-known commitment to the
  completing word too. Actual compliance is a different event.
- Distinguish available time (temporal_remaining) from event duration
  (temporal_duration), nominal route estimates, actual elapsed time and deadlines.
- Emit narrative_* features for supported thread/goal links, obstacles, visible
  changes, resolution evidence and possible closure. Use the new plot namespace
  for narrative function, cast roles, stakes and earlier-event links. Its role
  does not replace the outer action/patient/actor/location role. Interpretations
  are candidates, not engine-verified facts. There is no obligation to classify
  an archetype; [] is usually right before enough trajectory is visible.
- plot_id=plotN uses the earliest visible goal/issue word for that thread. Give
  every plot reading prefix evidence_indices. Use basis=prefix_interpretation,
  scope=observed_prefix, status=candidate. Narrative stage is a local reading,
  not proof that the story cannot continue. final_image and closure are only
  candidate readings grounded in resolved issues and a visible image; request
  position or end-of-file is not evidence. incoming_links must reference earlier
  evN snapshots, have basis=prefix_interpretation and mechanically_verified=false.
- Do not hallucinate native_dependencies, observed_consequences, source_scenario,
  simulator timestamps or mechanically checked completion from prose. Reuse
  goal_*, belief_*, temporal_*, narrative_* for their existing meanings; avoid
  generic synonyms like belief, expectation or role when the canonical key exists.

IDENTITY AND SCOPE CHECK
Search the ENTIRE supplied prefix for the first supported mention, including
possessives: Nia's before a later Nia is still the earlier person mention.
Do not anchor to the most recent name or pronoun. Titles before a proper name
are provisional mentions; once a name is known, prefer its first proper-name
head and use entity_merge_candidate if connecting a separately introduced title.
Do not merge different people merely because they share a name. Use an explicit
identity hypothesis/uncertainty when needed. A desired kind, a later encountered
animal, and its drawing are different referents.

Count quote openings from the visible spans. Closed quotations are not the parent
of a new quote. Preserve the current quote through nested negation, modality and
conditionals. Never use a speaker attribution that occurs after the target.
The speaker of an imperative is not its actor: the actor is a supported addressee,
or remains unresolved. Do not fill an unknown actor with the nearest person.
modal_auxiliary_class uses only can/may/must/shall/will/ought/need/dare, never
ability_family or possibility. Contextual modal force belongs in modal_base.

For literal pictures/reflections, use depiction, not symbolic_reference unless
an explained symbolic meaning is stated. Keep the image entity separate from
its physical carrier and an optional real-world referent. A sketchbook open to
an animal drawing does not put an actual animal inside the book. A depicted
marker does not share the real marker's entity_id. Preserve a depiction scope
on the current image and its events; do not copy physical locations into it.
