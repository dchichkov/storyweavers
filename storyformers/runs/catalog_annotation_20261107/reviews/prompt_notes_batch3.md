# Batch 3 review notes — not a prompt release

Frozen v3 remains the authoring prompt for all six stories 15–20. Do not release
the final revision until all six stories and their feedback are reviewed.

Accepted laundry review:

- Check quoted second-person reference against the addressee. A correct event
  actor and proposition do not compensate for an incorrect token entity.
- `Toward` describes direction or an approaching trajectory, not achieved
  arrival. Bind the motion feature to the actual phase.
- A container's return does not imply returning every associated item. The
  peg bag is returned while its pegs remain visible in use.
- Proposed pretense can contain a negated awareness claim without actual
  deception or actual unawareness. Preserve nested scope and the positive
  operator target separately.
- Distributed peg counts over specific corners do not establish a total
  inventory. Closing references to either member of a sock pair do not create
  additional socks.

Accepted trail review:

- Separate nominal route durations, reported time remaining and actual timely
  arrival. Do not infer actual elapsed duration from these alone.
- Mobility aids and a stated pace constraint do not establish a diagnosis or
  blanket inability. Keep each capacity bound to route, actor and conditions.
- A nearby route marker and a newly noticed triangle can remain distinct even
  when both are blue. Candidate identity is better than forced merging.
- Retain modal force in complete utterance content (`should be on our left`),
  while keeping bare positive content as the modal target.
- Pictured salamanders/markers and reflections must not move their physical
  referents. Preserve unreported compass handoffs between stated endpoints.
- Expand a group audience and exclude its speaker. A first-person plural action
  can include the speaker while the audience of the same utterance does not.
- `modal_auxiliary_class` is lexical (can/may/shall here); `modal_base` carries
  contextual ability, expectation or epistemic possibility. Optical reflection
  is not a directional `spatial_frame_kind`.
- A praise utterance is an actual outer speech event even when its trigger is
  a quoted noun such as `eyes`. Keep the quote and outer event scopes separate.
- An expected or prohibited spatial relation needs its operator and proposition
  inside its own bundle; quotation attribution alone does not retain that force.

Accepted repair review:

- Mechanical jamming belongs to operability. It does not imply engagement of a
  lock. Deliberately holding a wheel still during a test is a control condition.
- Distinguish the quoted diagnosis from its sensory evidence and the narrator's
  later explanation. Correcting nut looseness is not a logical negation of every
  possible wheel defect.
- Repeated mentions can refer to several earlier tightening turns without
  creating another physical action. Carrying a tool back for consultation is
  not itself a handoff.
- Qualitative life stage, size, weight and sunlight remain suggested future
  typed fields. Existing entity-bound legacy attributes suffice for this run;
  no new names have been admitted from those suggestions.

Accepted museum review:

- The desired absence of an asking event is not a desired asking event. Keep
  local negation inside the hope scope and retain positive operator targets.
- Converse loan perspectives use `retrieval_inverse_relation`; borrowing and
  lending are not metonymy. Keep the canonical event participants unchanged.
- In `modeling clay`, modeling is a nominal purpose modifier, not an activity
  being performed by the characters.
- Short reactions do not prove comprehension; comparison-sized shadows do not
  create physical tableware; possible attendance is not actual attendance.

Accepted biscuit review:

- Negating the reason for a rescue does not negate the rescue. Preserve the
  causal proposition as the operator target and the recalled event identity.
- Allocated gifts are not yet in the recipient's custody. A prospective unit
  may later be realized without retroactively fixing an earlier unchosen shape.
- Ingredient residue, ingredient batch, dough portions, cutters, biscuits and
  living animals remain separate. A surface impression is not a full cut-out.
- Required baking duration and remaining oven-entry margin have different roles
  despite sharing minute units. Cleaning tools does not imply returning them.
- Accept `symbolic_reference` provisionally in the next registry: an explicit
  artifact-symbol relation with carrier, represented entity/event, attributing
  person and scope. Existing shape/metonymy/metaphor/enactment fields do not
  replace it. The reviewed example uses `represents`, not inferred commemoration.

Accepted festival review and author feedback:

- Permission, choosing a position, tying a knot and attaching the chosen flag
  may belong to different people. A later last knot is not established merely
  because an earlier knot of the same kind was tied.
- Borrowed spool and used string are distinct. An evening ending does not prove
  the before-supper return deadline passed or that a promise was broken.
- Accessibility belongs to the named actor, task and route. Methods can recur
  in different teaching events without repeating the original wrapping action.
- A rejected purpose needs a negative reasoning conclusion while retaining the
  positive negation target. Restricted `each one` is not generic, and `every flag`
  still has singular noun morphology. A result connective is not temporal.
- Growing displays need time-qualified membership; later contributions must not
  become earlier actions by the initial participants. Preserve unresolved
  viewpoint referents and prefer the existing typed temperature field.

All six feedback files and independent story reviews have been read. All material
findings are corrected and verified. The release decision is in batch3_review.md;
these notes preserve the review trail rather than serving as the released prompt.
