## Lessons from the final six-story batch (v4)

Read `batch3_examples_v4.json` for corrected records from all six stories, and
use `extension_registry_v4.json` for the agreed provisional extensions. The
historical examples remain useful; their story-local IDs are never reusable
identities. The canonical catalog still contains 1,024 fields.

1. **Separate expression, operator and complete content.** Hoping nobody asks
   does not desire an asking event. Keep the negated action within the desired
   scope and retain a separate positive operator target. Rejecting a reason for
   a rescue does not reject the rescue itself. Expected or prohibited spatial
   relations must retain that force inside their bundles; quotation attribution
   alone is insufficient. Distinguish the complete prohibition, negative action
   content and positive action targeted by negation.
2. **Check names against definitions.** `modal_auxiliary_class` describes the
   lexical auxiliary; use normalized `can`, `may`, `shall` and so on consistently
   for new annotations, with contextual ability/expectation in `modal_base`.
   Older frozen examples may retain coarser lexical-family labels. Optical
   reflection is not a directional `spatial_frame_kind`. Borrow/lend perspectives
   are inverse relations, not metonymy. A result connective is not a temporal
   relation. A mechanically locked wheel is an
   operability problem, not necessarily an engaged lock. Deliberately holding it
   still is a control condition. Read purpose compounds such as `modeling clay`
   in context, and retain the participle after `might have passed`.
3. **Keep speakers, listeners and participants distinct.** Expand audience
   groups and exclude the speaker unless the text actually supports self-address.
   A first-person plural action can still include that speaker. Resolve quoted
   `you` to its actual addressee. A praise utterance can be an actual outer speech
   event whose lexical trigger is a quoted noun; use a secondary event binding
   and an explicit communication scope rather than moving the quoted word out
   of its quotation. A short reaction does not prove comprehension.
4. **Bind completion to the particular action.** Movement toward an entrance
   does not establish arrival. Returning a container does not return all its
   contents; cleaning borrowed tools does not establish their return. Carrying
   a tool back for consultation does not assert a handoff. Permission to tie,
   choosing a flag's position, tying a rail knot and attaching that flag can
   involve different actors. Performing a knot does not automatically fulfill
   a goal of tying the last knot. Preserve source gaps between stated endpoints.
5. **Distinguish quantities, phases and capabilities.** Nominal route durations,
   reported time remaining and timely arrival do not determine actual elapsed
   traversal time. Required baking duration and oven-entry slack are different
   quantities even when both use minutes. An evening ending alone does not prove
   a before-supper deadline passed. Bind accessibility to the particular person,
   task, route and conditions. Mobility aids and healing do not supply diagnoses
   or universal incapacity. A successful repair need not satisfy its original
   deadline or establish another character's trust.
6. **Preserve entities through material and representational changes.** Ingredient
   residue is a portion of a batch, not the entire batch. A torn and recombined
   lump can supply a biscuit while its material continuity stays explicit.
   Prospective shape choices are not already physical objects. Use bound
   variables for `not one` and `each one` over their stated domains; `each` over
   a particular flag display is not automatically generic reference. A growing
   group's members need time-qualified membership so earlier actions do not
   include later additions. Quantifying `every flag` does not make the noun's
   grammatical form plural. A cutter, shaped biscuit, shallow surface impression,
   picture and living animal remain
   separate. Reflections and drawings do not relocate their physical referents.
   Do not manufacture a missing handoff, unloading, removal or creation action
   merely because a later state presupposes one.
7. **Register a distinct meaning only when needed.** The new provisional
   `symbolic_reference` records an explicitly explained artifact symbol, such
   as a sugar star representing a flashlight. Its `carrier_id` is an artifact
   or decoration eN, `referent_id` is the represented eN entity or evN event,
   and `holder_id` is the person attributing the link. Preserve scope and
   assertion status; `represents` does not imply literal identity, performance
   enactment, ownership, or an unstated motive of commemoration. Qualitative
   size, life stage, weight, sunlight and ergonomic-fit suggestions remain
   feedback; use existing supported fields or entity-bound legacy attributes
   until a reusable typed definition is reviewed.

After correcting a field, check the same meaning's other occurrences in the
story, then regenerate output and any review view together. Evidence field lists
must describe the actual saved fields. Whole-story retrospective annotation,
synthetic nonwords and local positions, sparse field use, explicit uncertainty,
and separate semantic versus structural review remain unchanged.
