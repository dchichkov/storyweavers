# Batch 3 review and v4 release

All six stories, 15_repair through 20_biscuit, are accepted after complete story
reviews and verification of corrections. They contain 4,395 words. The complete
run contains 20 reviewed stories, 15,326 words and 34,367 lossless token records,
with zero structural validation errors or conflicting extension definitions.
Across the run 535 distinct canonical fields are used sparsely; this is observed
vocabulary coverage, not a target density or a claim of semantic exhaustiveness.

The independent reports are batch3_root_15.md, batch3_root_16.md,
batch3_root_17.md, batch3_independent_18.md, batch3_independent_19.md and
batch3_root_20.md. Reviewers read the complete source and assembled records with
the entity, event, scope and proposition tables. All six feedback files were
reviewed. Semantic judgments are documented separately from machine checks.

Corrections preserve the desired absence of an action, rejected reasons and
negative conclusions without reversing their positive operator targets. They
also separate lexical modality from contextual force, outer praise events from
quoted words, addressees from speakers, direction from achieved arrival, and
returning a container from returning its contents. Borrow/lend perspectives use
the inverse-relation field. Optical reflection is not a directional reference
frame, mechanical jamming is not an engaged lock, and a causal result is not a
temporal relation.

The festival review additionally separates permission, choosing, knot tying and
flag attachment, preserving their different actors. Its growing display has
time-qualified membership so later contributions do not enter earlier actions
or visibility claims. Bounded quantification does not become kind-level generic
reference or override a noun's singular grammatical form.

All stories retain documented source gaps and uncertain identities. Missing
handoffs, arrivals, tool returns, construction and elapsed durations are not
invented. Mobility and accessibility claims stay bound to the stated person,
task and conditions. A final successful action does not automatically prove a
deadline, broader goal or trust relationship was achieved.

The final prompts/annotate_v4.md incorporates these lessons and eight actual
reviewed examples in batch3_examples_v4.json. Each example includes its annotation
hash and the referenced story-local tables. extension_registry_v4.json retains
the previous six definitions and admits symbolic_reference for an explicitly
explained artifact symbol, preserving carrier, represented referent, attributing
person, scope and assertion status. It is distinct from shape, metaphor and
performance enactment. Qualitative size, life stage, weight, sunlight and
ergonomic-fit suggestions remain feedback. The canonical catalog stays at 1,024.

A bounded final consistency pass corrected seven modal-class labels in story 12
and one reflection-frame label in story 13, independently verified in
final_consistency_12_13.md. Earlier frozen examples remain unchanged and point to
their preserved reviewed snapshots through annotation_snapshots.json. The new
prompt specifies normalized lexical auxiliary labels for future annotations;
older frozen records can retain coarser lexical-family labels.

The requested three logical batches of six were maintained, with review and a
prompt revision after each. The session rejected a fourth simultaneous worker,
including the latest requested capacity probe, so work used three workers plus
the coordinator in waves. No external annotation API was called. This remains
whole-story retrospective data; prefix-only Luna annotation is a separate run.

The final completion audit checks all 20 reviewed stories, exact source and
catalog copies, prompt provenance, unchanged historical bundles, extension
consistency and all frozen examples. Its pass records mechanical completeness;
the reviews above record the semantic acceptance and remaining source limits.
