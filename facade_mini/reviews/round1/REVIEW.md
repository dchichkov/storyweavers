Preserved eleven scripted transcripts, three preselected curious-policy sessions
(seeds 0, 17, 41), and the source that generated them. These are development
samples, not holdouts. Read private, practical, betrayal, separation, and all
three curious sessions in full; the repair route was read directly at the terminal.

The private route now has a plausible emotional development, and the practical
route ends with observable commitments without requiring a reconciliation.
The following failures remained despite all fifteen mechanical tests passing:

- `tonal_reversal`: after a confidence was betrayed, the hosts resumed a sweet
  bicycle memory and several unrelated topics before reacting seriously. A breach
  now preempts the current topic and moves directly to the privacy crisis.
- `unearned_warmth`: betrayal continued through a shared dinner after the hosts
  had established that the guest was no longer welcome as an interpreter. That
  branch now has one focused aftermath scene, then departure.
- `redundant_decision`: the final beat asked the hosts to choose something after
  they had already made and enacted a choice. It now closes the evening instead
  of reopening the decision.
- `forgotten_event`: after Eli took responsibility autonomously, a later
  reflection prompt elicited the same confession and "first time" response.
  Reflection now checks actual accountability rather than only command history.
- `wrong_reentry`: a re-entry referred to an open brush roll before it had been
  brought to the table. The re-entry now references the ongoing conversation.
- `repetition`: repeated private silence produced the same glass-drying line.
  Three authored stages now lead the character to propose returning to the table.
- `wrong_apology`: apologizing after betrayal talked about judgment rather than
  breaking a confidence. Apology now names that recorded harm.
- `prop_drift`: calendar location and a key-check description did not match the
  claimed physical actions. Calendar movement is recorded; the key stays carried.

Further review must check repeated revelation across beats and topic mix-ins,
late changes of plan, and whether characters can abandon previously accepted
commitments without explanation. These are literary/state consistency concerns,
not questions answered by a large number of successful executions.
