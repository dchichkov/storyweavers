THE LAST PLACE SETTING

You arrive at Mara and Eli's flat for their anniversary dinner. They asked you, an old friend, to be their only guest.

Three plates wait under a low lamp. On the sideboard are a folded letter, a face-down receipt, and a photograph of the two of them outside an empty shop.

Eli takes your coat. "Perfect timing. We were just deciding what to celebrate."

Mara: "Our anniversary, Eli. That was the invitation."

> empathize mara

Mara: "I want to say something without having to defend it before I have finished."

Eli: "All right. I will wait until you have finished."

Mara pulls out the third chair. "Sit beside whoever you like. Apparently that matters tonight."

Eli: "It doesn't." He straightens the fork beside your plate without looking at her.

> talk mara

You ask Mara if you can help in the kitchen. Eli stays at the table.

Mara runs the tap until the water covers the sound of your voices. "For a minute, I need to be a person, not half of a couple."

> ask work

Mara stands beside the sink rather than facing you. "There is a restoration residency. Six months, starting Monday. I accepted it."

She watches a drop travel down the tap. "Eli doesn't know. I keep trying to find a version of the sentence that won't hurt him. There isn't one."

You can hear a chair move in the other room, but not Eli's voice.

> promise privacy

You: "I will let you tell your partner yourself. I won't carry the conversation back for you."

Mara: "Thank you. That makes it easier to think about actually doing it."

> return

You and Mara return to the dining room. Eli looks up but does not ask you to report the conversation.

Eli notices you looking at the white scar on his thumb. "That was her bicycle. The first time I fixed it."

Mara: "You refused money."

Eli: "You bought me dinner."

> tell mara

You: "Mara has accepted a residency. She is leaving on Monday."

Mara: "You promised you would let me say it."

Eli: "I needed to know. I didn't need you to do it like that."

Mara opens the paper on the sideboard with shaking hands. "There. Now everyone can look."

The broken confidence stops the earlier conversation. Neither host tries to return to it.

> apologize

You: "I broke a confidence. The truth being important did not make it mine to tell. I am sorry."

Mara: "I hear you. I need the next thing you do to be different."

Eli: "We invited you into our home. One of us tried to trust you with something difficult."

Mara: "And now the kitchen feels like another room where you have to prepare a defense."

> listen

Mara: "We do not get to make this all about our guest. We were keeping things from each other before the doorbell rang."

Eli: "I know. But I would like the next thing I hear to come from you."

> listen

They turn their chairs a fraction away from you and toward each other.

You can remain a witness. You have lost the right to be their interpreter.

> listen

Mara: "Before we talk about our promises to each other, I need to talk about yours to me."

She looks at you, not at Eli.

> listen

The other host does not come to your defense. Learning the truth has not made them grateful for the way it arrived.

Mara: "We still have to deal with what we did. You don't get to use that to make your part disappear."

> listen

Eli moves your glass a little closer to your side of the table.

Eli: "If there is another conversation after tonight, it won't happen with someone carrying messages between us."

No one asks you to answer.

> listen

Eli puts your glass on a tray. Mara looks toward the hall.

> listen

The hosts let the room grow quiet. You reach toward the back of your chair.

> listen

Eli turns on the hall light. It falls across the floor between the table and the door.

Mara: "We will finish this conversation ourselves." It is not a request for advice.

Eli brings your coat. He does not make a joke about the evening going wrong.

You leave them at the table. Through the closing door, you hear one of them ask the other a quiet question. This time you are not there to repeat the answer.

[Ending: broken_confidence]
