# Unfiltered first five seeds per tense

## library — seed 0, past

Mina held a moon atlas, and she hoped to borrow a moon atlas. Jun said that a whale book was missing, and she listened to Jun. Mina asked Ms Alvarez to lend Mina a book trolley, and Ms Alvarez lent Mina a book trolley. Mina placed the moon atlas on the front desk, and she held the book trolley. Mina and Jun searched the window nook, and Jun found a mitten, but Jun did not find the whale book. Mina and Jun searched the astronomy shelf with the book trolley, and Jun spotted a star bookmark on the high shelf. Mina asked Ms Alvarez to help Jun, and Ms Alvarez agreed to help Jun. Mina returned the moon atlas to the display table, and Jun thanked Mina. Ms Alvarez fetched a ladder from the storeroom. Ms Alvarez climbed the ladder, and she lifted the whale book, and Ms Alvarez stepped down. Jun saw the whale book, and the star bookmark was in the whale book. Ms Alvarez checked the whale book out, and she gave Jun the whale book. Mina returned the book trolley to the trolley station. Ms Alvarez returned the ladder to the storeroom. Jun held the whale book, and Mina and Jun read the whale book.
