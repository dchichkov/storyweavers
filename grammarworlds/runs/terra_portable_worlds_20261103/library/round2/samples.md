# Unfiltered first five seeds per tense

## library — seed 0, past

Mina held a moon atlas, and she hoped to borrow the atlas because Mina hoped to sketch craters for the class. Jun said that his whale book was missing, and Mina promised to help Jun. Mina borrowed a book trolley from Ms Alvarez. Mina placed the atlas on the front desk. Mina and Jun searched the window nook, and Jun placed a mitten on the book trolley, but Mina did not find the whale book in the window nook. Mina and Jun searched the astronomy shelf with the book trolley, and Jun saw a star bookmark on the high shelf. Mina asked Ms Alvarez to help Jun. Mina placed the atlas on the display table, and she chose to help Jun. Ms Alvarez fetched the ladder from the storeroom. Ms Alvarez climbed the ladder, and she lifted the whale book from the high shelf, and Ms Alvarez stepped down. Jun recognized the blue whale book, and the star bookmark was in the book. Ms Alvarez checked the whale book out to Jun. Ms Alvarez returned the ladder to the storeroom. Mina placed the book trolley at the trolley station. Jun opened the whale book, and Mina and Jun read together.
