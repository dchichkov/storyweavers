## pottery — seed 0, past

Grandpa Len's hand was stiff, and Grandpa Len struggled to hold the old mug's thin handle. Bea hoped to give Grandpa Len the cup, and the cup's wide handle was for two fingers. Bea used Mr Ivo's borrowed shaping rib to shape the cup. The shaping rib slipped, and the cup was dented. Mr Ivo inspected the cup's wall, and the wall was thick. Bea fetched the sponge from the bench, and she hoped to smooth the cup's dent. Bea smoothed the cup's dent with the sponge, and the cup's handle was wide. Bea returned the sponge to the bench. Bea returned the shaping rib to Mr Ivo. Bea carried the cup from the bench to the shelf. The cup dried on the shelf. Mr Ivo carried the cup from the shelf to the kiln. Mr Ivo used the kiln to bisque the cup. The cup cooled in the kiln. Mr Ivo carried the cup from the kiln to the bench. Bea used blue glaze to glaze the cup. Mr Ivo carried the cup from the bench to the kiln. Mr Ivo fired the cup in the kiln. The cup cooled in the kiln. Mr Ivo brought Bea the cup. Bea gave Grandpa Len the cup. Grandpa Len poured tea from the teapot in the cup. Grandpa Len held the cup's handle, and the cup was blue.
