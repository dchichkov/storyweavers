Nia accepted his answer and played a soft beat, leaving space where another rhythm might have entered. On Friday, she listed herself alone for a drum solo on the signup sheet, though she had imagined another name beside hers. The two drums stood on the shelf with the red ribbon between them, while the last notes of Nia's solo faded in the quiet room.
