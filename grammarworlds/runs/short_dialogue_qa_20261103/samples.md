## Rescue

A cold puppy was on a high ledge. Nora said, “I want to rescue the puppy.” Dad said, “The ladder is in the shed.” Nora lifted the puppy down with the ladder. Nora wrapped the puppy in the warm blanket. The puppy rested in the warm blanket.

**Q: What problem begins the story?**

The puppy was on a high ledge. It was cold.

> Evidence: A cold puppy was on a high ledge. Nora said, “I want to rescue the puppy.”

**Q: Who wanted to solve the problem?**

Nora wanted to solve it. Nora wanted to rescue the puppy.

> Evidence: A cold puppy was on a high ledge. Nora said, “I want to rescue the puppy.”

**Q: Where was the ladder?**

The ladder was in the shed. Dad told Nora where to find it.

> Evidence: Dad said, “The ladder is in the shed.”

**Q: How was the problem solved?**

Nora used the ladder. Nora lifted the puppy down.

> Evidence: Nora lifted the puppy down with the ladder.

**Q: What happened at the end?**

The puppy rested in the warm blanket. It was warm at the end.

> Evidence: The puppy rested in the warm blanket.

**Q: What did Nora use to warm the puppy?**

Nora wrapped the puppy in a blanket. The blanket was warm.

> Evidence: Nora wrapped the puppy in the warm blanket.
