# Event-pool samples

Seeds 0–4, word heads on, no filtering.

## Seed 0 — hat.stage

Teo wanted to see a trick. The brown rabbit waited in the pen. Ada raised a velvet hat. Ada said, “I promise I'll show you a surprise.” Teo closed his eyes. Ada placed the brown rabbit inside the hat. Teo opened his eyes. Two ears appeared above the hat. Ada lifted the brown rabbit out of the hat. Teo laughed. Ada placed the brown rabbit and the hat on the desk. She put the carrot beside the brown rabbit. The brown rabbit nibbled the carrot beside the open hat.
