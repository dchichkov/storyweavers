Seeds 0–4, word heads on, no filtering.

## Seed 0 — hat.portal_bound

June wanted to see a trick. The rabbit waited in the hutch. Merlin raised a magician's hat. Merlin said, “I promise to show you a surprise.” With the wand, he tapped the hat. The rabbit stayed in the hutch. June spotted the tight ribbon around the hat. Merlin untied the ribbon. He used the wand to tap the hat again. The rabbit disappeared from the hutch. Two ears appeared above the hat. Merlin lifted the rabbit out of the hat. June laughed. Merlin put the rabbit and the hat on the table. He placed the carrot beside the rabbit. The rabbit nibbled the carrot beside the open hat.
