Seeds 0–4, word heads on, no filtering.

## Seed 0 — hat.stage

June wanted to see a trick. The rabbit waited in the hutch. Merlin raised a top hat. Merlin said, “I promise to show you a surprise.” June closed her eyes. Merlin put the rabbit inside the hat. June opened her eyes. Two ears appeared above the hat. Merlin pulled the rabbit out of the hat. June laughed. Merlin put the rabbit and the hat on the table. Merlin placed the carrot beside the rabbit. The rabbit nibbled the carrot beside the open hat.
