## rescue — off

Ben wanted to eat the bread, but a puppy shivered on a high ledge. Ben said, “The puppy needs help.” Mum said, “I can lower the basket.” Ben said, “I can share the bread.” Ben put the bread in the basket. Mum carried the basket to the window, and she lowered the basket to the ledge with the rope. Ben waited because the puppy was frightened. The puppy entered the basket, and it ate the bread. Mum lowered the basket to the garden with the rope. Ben carried the puppy into the cottage, and he wrapped the puppy in the warm blanket. Ben was hungry, but the puppy rested in the warm blanket.

A: The puppy ate the bread that Ben had meant to eat. It rested in the warm blanket after the rescue.

Q: Who noticed that the animal needed help?
A: Ben said the puppy needed help.

Q: What did they offer to do?
A: Mum offered to lower a basket. Ben offered to share the bread.

Q: Where did the bread go?
A: Ben put the bread in the basket.

Q: Why did Ben wait?
A: Ben waited because the puppy was frightened.

## key — off

Ben unlocked the door with the spare key, and he stepped inside the cottage. He returned the spare key to Mum. Ben said, “I promise to look for the key tomorrow.” He wrote a note, and he hung the note on the hook. The key was missing, but he opened the storybook beside Mum.

Q: What was solved, and what still needed doing?

Q: Who wanted to read inside?

A: The door was locked, and the key was missing.

Q: How did Mum help after Ben admitted the loss?
A: Mum gave Ben a spare key. Ben had admitted losing the original key.

Q: Where was the reminder?
A: The note was on the hook. Ben had promised to look for the missing key tomorrow.

Q: Who offered help?
A: Mum offered to help Ben.

Nia secured the umbrella above the tomato seedlings with the garden tie. She moved the sunflower under the umbrella. Mr Bell said, “I promise to repair the tap tomorrow.” The tomato seedlings and the sunflower stood upright under the umbrella.

A: The rain barrel held one can of water.

A: Nia chose to divide the water equally. There was only one can of water in the barrel.

A: The shade cloth had fallen. Nia secured an umbrella above the seedlings with a garden tie.

## pottery — off

Mr Ivo said, “The cup's wall is thick.” Grandpa Len said, “I can hold a bowl with two hands.” Bea said, “I want a thumb rest.” Bea used the sponge to shape the dent into a thumb rest. Mr Ivo fired the cup twice. The cup was cool. Bea gave Grandpa Len the cup. Grandpa Len poured tea into the cup. Grandpa Len's thumb rested in the thumb rest.

Q: How did Bea use the dent after Mr Ivo checked the cup?
A: Bea shaped the dent into a thumb rest with the sponge. Mr Ivo had said the cup's wall was thick.

A: Grandpa Len rested his thumb in the thumb rest. Bea had shaped the dent into that thumb rest.

Q: Who was Bea making the cup for?
A: Bea was making the cup for Grandpa Len.

Q: Why did the cup have a wide handle?
A: Grandpa Len's hand was stiff. The cup had a wide handle for him.

Q: What happened when the shaping rib slipped?
A: The cup was dented when the shaping rib slipped.

Q: What did Mr Ivo say about the cup's wall?
A: Mr Ivo said the cup's wall was thick.
