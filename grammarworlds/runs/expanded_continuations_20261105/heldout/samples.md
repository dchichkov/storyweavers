Bea said, “I want to give Grandpa Len a cup.” The cup's handle was wide because Grandpa Len's hand was stiff. The shaping rib slipped, and the cup was dented. Mr Ivo said, “The cup's wall is thick.” Grandpa Len said, “I can hold a bowl with two hands.” Bea said, “I want a bowl.” Bea reshaped the cup into a bowl with the shaping rib. Mr Ivo fired the bowl twice. The bowl was cool. Bea gave Grandpa Len the bowl. Grandpa Len poured tea into the bowl. Grandpa Len held the bowl with two hands.

Q: How did Bea change her gift design?
A: Bea reshaped the cup into a bowl with the shaping rib. Grandpa Len had said he could hold a bowl with two hands.

Q: What did Grandpa Len do with the bowl at the end?

Q: Who was Bea making the cup for?
A: Bea was making the cup for Grandpa Len.
