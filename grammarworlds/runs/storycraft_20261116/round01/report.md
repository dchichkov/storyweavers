## pottery / seed 7 / sampled

A Gift for Grandpa Len

Bea had made Grandpa Len a cup for his birthday. She wanted it straight and smooth, just like the drawing. But the shaping rib slipped. A dent appeared below the handle of the cup. Bea turned that side toward the wall. Bea said, “I could start again.” Mr Ivo said, “You can. But a new cup won't be ready for Saturday.” Grandpa Len came to collect her. Bea moved the sponge aside. Bea said, “Your present has gone wrong. Will you help me?” Grandpa Len's stiff fingers would not curl around his old mug's narrow handle. Grandpa Len said, “My thumb needs somewhere here.” Bea said, “Where the dent is?” Bea looked at her drawing, then put it away. With the sponge, she rounded the dent into a place for his thumb. After drying, glazing, two firings and a final cooling, the cup was ready on Saturday. Bea set the cup on Grandpa Len's table with the dent facing him. Grandpa Len filled the cup with tea. With his thumb in the hollow, he lifted it in one hand.

The End.
