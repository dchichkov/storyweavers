The Cup in the Drawing

The Cup

Once upon a time, there was someone named Bea.

Bea told Mr Ivo, “I would like to give Grandpa Len a cup.” The cup's handle was wide because Grandpa Len's hand was stiff. The shaping rib slipped. The cup was dented. Mr Ivo said, “The cup's wall is thick.” Grandpa Len told Bea, “I could hold a bowl with two hands.” Bea said, “I want to smooth out the dent.” With the sponge, Bea smoothed the dent. Mr Ivo fired the cup twice. The cup was cool. Bea handed Grandpa Len the cup. Grandpa Len poured tea into the cup. Grandpa Len still had a stiff hand. Grandpa Len held the cup's wide handle.

Q: What did Bea want her present to be like?
A: Bea wanted a straight, smooth cup like her drawing. It was meant to be a birthday surprise for Grandpa Len.

Q: Why would starting again be difficult?
A: Mr Ivo said a new cup would not be ready for Saturday. Remaking it would mean missing Grandpa Len’s birthday.

Q: What did Bea give up when she asked for help?
A: Bea showed Grandpa the unfinished present and asked for help. He now knew what the surprise was.

Q: What did Grandpa want Bea to change?
A: Grandpa needed a place for his thumb because his hand was stiff. He indicated the dent below the handle.

Q: Why did Bea keep the dent?
A: Grandpa showed her where his thumb needed support. Bea made that dent into a hollow instead of making the cup match her drawing.

Q: How did the changed cup help Grandpa?
A: His thumb rested in the hollow Bea had made from the dent. He could hold his tea in one hand.
