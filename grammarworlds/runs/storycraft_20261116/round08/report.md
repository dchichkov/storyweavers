Nia tied the yellow ribbon around the sunflower pot for the show at two. The show trolley waited by the door. The tomato seedlings drooped in their tray inside the greenhouse. One leaf had already curled. The broken tap gave no water. Mr Bell said, “One can left in the barrel. No repair part until tomorrow.” Mr Bell handed over the watering can. Nia filled it from the barrel. Mr Bell said, “The little class planted those.” Nia said, “Will half a can be enough?” Mr Bell said, “It may be enough, with shade. You must stay to keep moving the umbrella.” Mr Bell said, “Their bus is due. I am going with them on the afternoon trip.” Nia looked at the trolley, then poured half the water over the seedlings and half into the sunflower pot. The torn shade cloth fell, leaving the tray in sunlight. Nia opened her umbrella above the tray. Its handle slid sideways. Mr Bell hurried to the gate to meet the bus. At two, Nia was still turning the umbrella to follow the shade. The show had begun without her. The trolley stood unused. Under the umbrella, the yellow ribbon brushed the lifted seedling leaves.
