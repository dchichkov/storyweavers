## greenhouse / seed 19 / choose_share

The Tale of Nia

Once upon a time, Nia was at the greenhouse.

Nia tied the yellow ribbon around the sunflower pot for the show at two. The show trolley waited by the door. The tomato seedlings drooped in their tray inside the greenhouse. The broken tap gave no water. Mr Bell said, “One can left in the barrel. No repair part until tomorrow.” Mr Bell handed over the watering can. Nia filled it from the barrel. Mr Bell said, “The little class planted those.” Nia said, “Will half a can be enough?” Mr Bell said, “With shade. You must stay to keep moving the umbrella.” Nia looked at the trolley, then poured half the water over the seedlings and half into the sunflower pot. The torn shade cloth slipped from its hook. Sunlight reached the tray. Nia opened her umbrella above the tray. Its handle slid sideways. Nia said, “Hold it while I tie it.” Mr Bell caught the handle and held it straight. Nia tied the handle to the heavy pot. Together they moved the flower beside the tray. At two, Nia was still turning the umbrella to follow the shade. The show had begun without her. Nia returned the empty can to Mr Bell. The trolley stood unused. Under the umbrella, the yellow ribbon brushed the lifted seedling leaves.

The End.
