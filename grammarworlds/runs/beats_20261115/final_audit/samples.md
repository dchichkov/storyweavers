## bakery: seed1103, past, budget2

The Birthday Cake

Once upon a time, Tessa was at the bakery.

Mr Hobb was lonely. Tessa told Mrs Pike, “I want to give the birthday cake to Mr Hobb.” Mrs Pike told Tessa, “I need the wicker basket.” Tessa borrowed the wicker basket. The birthday cake was in the wicker basket. Tessa waited beside the paved path. The birthday cake's icing was smudged. Tessa put the birthday cake on the kitchen table. Mr Hobb said, “I am pleased.” He lit the candle. Tessa returned the wicker basket to Mrs Pike. Mr Hobb opened the window. The candle glowed.

And that is where this story ends.

Selected beats: none.

Q: What changed at Mr Hobb’s window?
A: Mr Hobb opened his window. The birthday candle glowed beside him.

Q: Who was the birthday cake for?
A: The birthday cake was for Mr Hobb. Tessa wanted to give it to him.

Q: What did Mrs Pike need back?
A: Mrs Pike needed her wicker basket back. Tessa was borrowing it to carry the cake.

Q: How did Tessa carry the cake?
A: Tessa borrowed a wicker basket. The cake traveled in it.

Q: Where did Tessa put the cake?
A: Tessa put the cake on Mr Hobb’s kitchen table. She had carried it in the basket.

Q: Did the imperfect cake spoil the visit?
A: Mr Hobb was pleased with the cake. Tessa had put it on his table.

The Tomato Seedlings

Once upon a time, there was someone named Nia.

Nia wanted to water the sunflower. But the tomato seedlings were thirsty. Mr Bell said, “The tap is broken.” The rain barrel held one can of water. Nia told Mr Bell, “I want to water the sunflower first.” With the watering can, Nia watered the sunflower. The shade cloth fell. The tomato seedlings had a brown leaf. Nia secured the umbrella above the tomato seedlings with the garden tie. Mr Bell fixed the tap. Nia watered the tomato seedlings with water from the tap. With the clip, Mrs Okafor fixed the shade cloth. Nia said, “I promise to water the tomato seedlings first tomorrow.” The brown leaf remained under the repaired shade cloth.

And that is where this story ends.
