## rescue

Sam wanted to eat the bread, but a puppy shivered on a high ledge. Sam told Mum, “The puppy needs help.” Mum told Sam, “Let me climb the ladder.” Sam told Mum, “I can steady the ladder.” Sam fetched the ladder from the shed, and he steadied the ladder. Mum climbed the ladder. She lifted the puppy down. Sam carried the puppy into the cottage, and he wrapped the puppy in the warm blanket. The puppy rested in the warm blanket.

Q: Where did the rescued animal rest?
A: The puppy rested in the warm blanket. Mum had lifted it down while Sam steadied the ladder.

Q: What interrupted the meal?
A: A puppy was shivering on a high ledge. Sam wanted to eat the bread.

Q: Who noticed that the animal needed help?
A: Sam said the puppy needed help.

Q: How did they divide the rescue work?
A: Mum would climb the ladder. Sam would steady it.

Q: Where did the ladder come from?
A: Sam fetched the ladder from the shed.

Q: How did the animal get down?
A: Mum climbed the ladder and lifted the puppy down. Sam steadied the ladder.
