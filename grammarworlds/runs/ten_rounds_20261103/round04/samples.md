# Inspected development samples

## key / seed 0

Ben wanted to read a storybook inside, but the door was locked. The key was missing. Mum said, “I can help you.” Ben said, “I lost the key.” Mum gave Ben a spare key. Ben unlocked the door with the spare key, and he stepped inside the cottage. Ben returned the spare key to Mum. Ben said, “I promise to look for the key tomorrow.” Ben wrote a note, and he hung the note on the hook. The key was missing, but Ben opened the storybook beside Mum.

Q: What was solved, and what still needed doing?
A: Ben got inside and opened the storybook beside Mum. The original key was still missing; Ben promised to look tomorrow.

Q: Who wanted to read inside?
A: Ben wanted to read a storybook inside. The door was locked.

Q: Why could they not open the door?
A: The door was locked, and the key was missing.

Q: How did Mum help after Ben admitted the loss?
A: Mum gave Ben a spare key. Ben had admitted losing the original key.

Q: Where was the reminder?
A: The note was on the hook. Ben had promised to look for the missing key tomorrow.

Q: Who offered help?
A: Mum offered to help Ben.

## key / seed 7

Nora wanted to read a storybook inside, but the door was locked. The key was missing. Dad said, “I can help you.” Nora said, “I want to find the key.” Nora fetched the lantern from the porch. Nora used the lantern to find the key under the bench. Nora unlocked the door with the key, and she stepped inside the cottage. Nora hung the key on the hook. Nora opened the storybook beside Dad.

Q: What changed by the end?
A: Nora opened the storybook beside Dad. The found key was on the hook.

Q: Who wanted to read inside?
A: Nora wanted to read a storybook inside. The door was locked.

Q: Why could they not open the door?
A: The door was locked, and the key was missing.

Q: Where was the lantern?
A: Nora fetched the lantern from the porch.

Q: How was the missing key found?
A: Nora found it under the bench with the lantern. The lantern came from the porch.

Q: Who offered help?
A: Dad offered to help Nora.
