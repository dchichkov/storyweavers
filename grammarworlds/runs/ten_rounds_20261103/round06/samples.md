# Inspected development samples

## greenhouse / seed 0

Nia wanted to water the sunflower, but the tomato seedlings were thirsty. Mr Bell said, “The tap is broken.” The rain barrel held one can of water. Nia filled the watering can from the rain barrel. Nia said, “I can divide water equally.” Nia divided water equally between the tomato seedlings and the sunflower. The shade cloth fell. Nia secured the umbrella above the tomato seedlings with the garden tie. Nia moved the sunflower under the umbrella. Mr Bell said, “I promise to repair the tap tomorrow.” The tomato seedlings and the sunflower stood under the umbrella.

Q: How were the plants protected at the end?
A: The tomato seedlings and sunflower stood under the umbrella. Nia had divided the water equally between them.

Q: Who wanted to water the sunflower?
A: Nia wanted to water the sunflower. The tomato seedlings were thirsty too.

Q: What was wrong with the water supply?
A: Mr Bell said the tap was broken.

Q: Where did Nia get the first water?
A: Nia filled the watering can from the rain barrel. The barrel held one can of water.

Q: How did Nia choose to use the limited water?
A: Nia chose to divide the water equally. There was only one can of water in the barrel.

Q: Why did Nia secure an umbrella above the seedlings?
A: The shade cloth had fallen. Nia secured an umbrella above the seedlings with a garden tie.
