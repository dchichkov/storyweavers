# Inspected development samples

## rescue / seed 0

Ben wanted to eat the bread, but a puppy shivered on a high ledge. Ben said, “The puppy needs help.” Mum said, “I can lower the basket.” Ben said, “I can share the bread.” Ben put the bread in the basket. Mum carried the basket to the window, and she lowered the basket to the ledge with the rope. Ben waited because the puppy was frightened. The puppy entered the basket, and it ate the bread. Mum lowered the basket to the garden with the rope. Ben carried the puppy into the cottage, and he wrapped the puppy in the warm blanket. Ben was hungry, but the puppy rested in the warm blanket.

Q: Why was Ben hungry at the end?
A: The puppy ate the bread that Ben had meant to eat. It rested in the warm blanket after the rescue.

Q: What interrupted the meal?
A: A puppy was shivering on a high ledge. Ben wanted to eat the bread.

Q: Who noticed that the animal needed help?
A: Ben said the puppy needed help.

Q: What did they offer to do?
A: Mum offered to lower a basket. Ben offered to share the bread.

Q: Where did the bread go?
A: Ben put the bread in the basket.

Q: Why did Ben wait?
A: The puppy was frightened. Ben waited.
