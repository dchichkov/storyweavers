## rescue

All 20 stories use the same basic premise: a small animal is stranded on a high ledge and a child helps rescue it. The set has two meaningful rescue mechanisms: a ladder steadied by the child (for example, s000028 and s000034), or a bread-filled basket lowered on a rope to coax down a frightened animal (for example, s000014 and s000041). Both lead to the animal being brought inside and warmed, with largely repeated language. This describes only this sample.

Causal plot groups:

- 10 stories: A child shares bread in a basket lowered by an adult on a rope; the frightened stranded animal enters, is lowered to safety, and is taken inside to a warm blanket.
- 10 stories: The child fetches and steadies a ladder while an adult climbs up and lifts the stranded animal down; the child takes it inside and wraps it in a warm blanket.

- s000058 (8/9): Clear ladder-rescue sequence and a gentle conclusion, though the final moment could be more vivid.

## Scope

Each diversity score describes 20 sampled positions per scenario, not the full generator. These authored situations share much of their machinery. No cross-scenario semantic diversity score is claimed. Exact unique strings and different event-kind sequences do not establish different plots; optional goal/thanks beats can inflate path counts. Trigram overlap and repeated sentences are available in mechanical.json; they measure surface reuse, not quality. One Luna judgment per set is a diagnostic baseline, not an independently calibrated human evaluation or confidence interval. Requests, untouched samples, responses, token usage, and source snapshots are saved for audit. No price estimate is claimed.
