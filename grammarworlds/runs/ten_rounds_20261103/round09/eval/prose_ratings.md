## Scope

Each diversity score describes 10 sampled positions per scenario, not the full generator. These authored situations share much of their machinery. No cross-scenario semantic diversity score is claimed. Exact unique strings and different event-kind sequences do not establish different plots; optional goal/thanks beats can inflate path counts. Trigram overlap and repeated sentences are available in mechanical.json; they measure surface reuse, not quality. One Luna judgment per set is a diagnostic baseline, not an independently calibrated human evaluation or confidence interval. Requests, untouched samples, responses, token usage, and source snapshots are saved for audit. No price estimate is claimed.
