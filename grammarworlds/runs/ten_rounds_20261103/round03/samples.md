## kite / seed 0

Ben tied the sail to the boat with the string. Ben set the boat on the pond. Ben and Mum watched the boat. The boat sailed across the pond.

## kite / seed 7

Nora launched the kite in the wind. Nora and Dad watched the patched kite. The kite danced above the meadow.

Q: What did they watch at the end?
A: Nora and Dad watched the patched kite dancing above the meadow. The tape had covered its tear.

Q: Who wanted to fly the kite?
A: Nora wanted to fly the kite. Its paper was torn.

Q: What stopped the kite from flying?
A: The kite was torn.

Q: How did Nora mend the kite?
A: Nora covered the tear with tape.

Q: Where did the repaired kite fly?
A: The kite danced above the meadow.

Q: What did Dad say the boat needed?
A: Dad said the boat needed a sail.
