## pottery / seed 0

Bea said, “I want to give Grandpa Len a cup.” The cup's handle was wide because Grandpa Len's hand was stiff. The shaping rib slipped, and the cup was dented. Mr Ivo said, “The cup's wall is thick.” Bea said, “I want a thumb rest.” Bea used the sponge to shape the dent into a thumb rest. Mr Ivo fired the cup twice. The cup was cool. Bea gave Grandpa Len the cup. Grandpa Len poured tea into the cup. Grandpa Len's thumb rested in the thumb rest.

Q: How did Bea use the dent?
A: Bea shaped the dent into a thumb rest with the sponge. Mr Ivo had said the cup's wall was thick.

Q: Who was Bea making the cup for?
A: Bea was making the cup for Grandpa Len.

Q: Why did the cup have a wide handle?
A: Grandpa Len's hand was stiff. The cup had a wide handle for him.

Q: What happened when the shaping rib slipped?
A: The shaping rib slipped. The cup was dented.

Q: Where did Grandpa Len rest his thumb?
A: Grandpa Len rested his thumb in the thumb rest. Bea had shaped the dent into that thumb rest.

Q: What did Mr Ivo say about the cup's wall?
A: Mr Ivo said the cup's wall was thick.
