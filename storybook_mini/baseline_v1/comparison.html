A Light at the Landing

full
14 facts · 11 sentences · 3 pronouns · 3 combinations

Once upon a time, Mara lived in a cottage and had a brass lantern. Oren needed the brass lantern at a river landing. Mara lit the brass lantern and carried it to the river landing.

On the riverbank, the wind blew out the brass lantern. Mara felt afraid. Oren said, “Cup your hands around the flame.” Mara shielded the brass lantern and kindled it.

At the landing, Mara hung the brass lantern on an iron hook. Oren thanked her. Mara felt calm again. As night fell, the brass lantern shone over the river landing.

The Kite with a Blue Patch

full
15 facts · 12 sentences · 3 pronouns · 3 combinations

One windy afternoon, Tomas had a red kite and walked to a grassy hill. He met Ada and launched the red kite.

A moment later, the red kite caught on a low branch. Tomas took down the red kite from the low branch. Because the red kite was torn, Tomas felt miserable. Ada said, “We can mend it together.”

Ada gave a blue patch to Tomas. Tomas mended the red kite with the blue patch. He thanked Ada.

Before sunset, Tomas sent up the red kite. He felt cheerful again. The blue patch glowed against the evening sky.
