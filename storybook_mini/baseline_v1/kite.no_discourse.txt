One windy afternoon, Tomas had the red kite and walked to the grassy hill. Tomas met Ada and launched the red kite.

A moment later, the red kite caught on the low branch. Tomas took down the red kite from the low branch. Because the red kite was torn, Tomas felt miserable. Ada said, “We can mend it together.”

Ada gave the blue patch to Tomas. Tomas mended the red kite with the blue patch. Tomas thanked Ada.

Before sunset, Tomas sent up the red kite. Tomas felt cheerful again. The blue patch glowed against the evening sky.
