Once upon a time, Mara lived in a cottage. She had a brass lantern. Oren needed it at a river landing. Mara lit the brass lantern. She carried it to the river landing.

On the riverbank, the wind blew out the brass lantern. Mara felt afraid. Oren said, “Cup your hands around the flame.” Mara shielded the brass lantern. She kindled it.

At the landing, Mara hung the brass lantern on an iron hook. Oren thanked her. Mara felt calm again. As night fell, the brass lantern shone over the river landing.
