Once upon a time, Nell lived in the small cottage. Nell wanted to bake the plaited loaf. Ada had the folded invitation.

“Iris is leaving tomorrow; a warm loaf might persuade her to stay for supper,” Nell said to Ada. Nell took the wicker basket. Nell took the white cloth.

“A basket is a hopeful start for a loaf we have not baked,” Ada said to Nell.

Nell walked with Ada to the stone workshop.

Iris stood beside the garden gate. Iris had the brass lamp.

“May we borrow your lamp until six; our oven room has no light?” Nell said to Iris.

“I need the brass lamp for the cellar steps,” Iris said to Nell. “Leave one measure of oil for the steps; they are steep enough in daylight,” Iris said to Nell. Iris lent the brass lamp to Nell.

“I will return the brass lamp to you,” Nell said to Iris. Nell carried the brass lamp with Ada to the windowless kitchen.

Ada put the white cloth on the wooden table. Nell checked the glass reservoir. The glass reservoir was almost empty. Nell checked the brass wheel.

The brass wheel was stiff.

“That little wheel has the manners of a locked front door,” Ada said to Nell.

Nell turned the brass wheel.

Nell wanted to bake the plaited loaf. However, Nell did not light the brass lamp.

“If the lamp stays cold, is this still a surprise supper?” Ada said to Nell.

“The last measure belongs to her steps; a loaf cannot make them less steep,” Nell said to Ada. Nell closed the brick oven. As a result, Nell felt miserable.

“Then let us ask about breakfast instead; there is room on the invitation,” Ada said to Nell.

Nell carried the brass lamp with Ada to the stone workshop.

“We hoped to give you supper; may we bring a loaf tomorrow, if you are still here?” Nell said to Iris.

“Ask me in the morning; the van has not told me its plans either,” Iris said to Nell.

Nell returned the brass lamp to Iris. Iris checked the brass lamp. The brass lamp was unlit. Ada gave the folded invitation to Iris.

As a result, Iris smiled.

“I will keep the folded invitation,” Iris said to Ada.

At six, Iris lit the brass lamp. Iris walked to the cellar steps. The plaited loaf was unbaked. The brass lamp shone over the cellar steps.
