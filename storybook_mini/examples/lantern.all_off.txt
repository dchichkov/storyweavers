Once upon a time, Mara lived in the cottage. Mara had the brass lantern. Oren needed the brass lantern at the river landing. Mara lit the brass lantern.

Mara carried the brass lantern to the river landing.

On the riverbank, the wind blew out the brass lantern. As a result, Mara felt afraid.

Oren said, “Cup your hands around the flame.”

Mara shielded the brass lantern. Mara lit the brass lantern.

At the landing, Mara hung the brass lantern on the iron hook. As a result, Oren thanked Mara. Mara felt calm again. As night fell, the brass lantern shone over the river landing.
