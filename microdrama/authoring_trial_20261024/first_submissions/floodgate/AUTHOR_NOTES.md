# The Weight Beneath the Water

A mill worker must protect forty sacks while a newcomer is blamed for a jammed floodgate. A physical diagram supplies a lifting-and-bracing memeplex; divided attention halves both transferred terms. A small sluice offers a real practice outcome before the heavier gate requires another pair of hands. Ren's bandaged wrist prevents him from substituting for Osk in that heavier action.

Seven bounded encounters run in a deliberate order. They separate the community's accusation, Ida's observed cause, learned procedure, applied procedure, and gate outcome. Observation clears Ren in every configuration without changing the bent pin. Opening bypasses the bent pin; it does not straighten it. The borrowed lever always belongs to Osk and is returned. The validator requires applied full knowledge and actual assistance for an open gate. Saved versus lost grain is a material counterfactual (40 versus 24 dry sacks), as are water level and gate state.
