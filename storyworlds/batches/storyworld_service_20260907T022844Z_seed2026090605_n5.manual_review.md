# Luna Pilot: Manual Review And Historical Comparison

Reviewed 2026-09-06 local time (2026-09-07 UTC). Review only: no generated
scripts, repair rules, prompts, model defaults, or historical ratings changed.
No additional API calls were made.

## Review Findings

### P1: A Fifth Of The Batch Still Cannot Be Parsed

[Bravery, line 249](../worlds/gpt-5.6-luna_service_20260907T022844Z_seed2026090605_n5/naked_bravery_friendship_nursery_rhyme.py#L249)
contains an unterminated string. The ordinary repair attempt failed and was
rolled back. This is not a model truncation: the API response completed after
3,432 output tokens. The story cannot be exported as-is.

Static reading also reveals an independent object-continuity problem: the
friend registry offers a robe, sweater, or towel, but the action and final
physical state always use a robe (lines 227-257). The setting may be a playroom,
but the fixed opening still invokes the morning breeze and tall grass. Fixing
the quote alone would not establish story quality. No runtime quality score
is assigned to this world.

### P1: High-Volume Variation Still Does Not Mean Different Stories

The Nose and Bury worlds have one unconditional narrative event sequence.
Dining has three search approaches, but every approach reaches the same fixed
discovery. The recorded emotions mostly accompany the text rather than
determine what can happen. This remains the central dataset-quality problem,
not a syntax problem.

Concrete controlled checks, using the same resolved parameters and changing
one field at a time, produced identical story text AND identical story QA:

| World | Changed parameter | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Dining | `mood`: curious to thoughtful | No story/QA change |
| Nose | `trait`: one supported trait to another | No story/QA change |
| Bury | `trait`: patient to curious | No story/QA change |
| Bury | `partner`: Milo to DistinctName | No story/QA change |

For example, [Bury's turn](../worlds/gpt-5.6-luna_service_20260907T022844Z_seed2026090605_n5/bury_teamwork_fable.py#L205)
always divides the work in exactly the same way. Changing the animal does not
change its contribution. This is much less diverse than the recent manually
repaired multi-arc worlds, although those are a curated, intervention-heavy
reference set, not a fair raw-model control.

### P2: Pipeline Sampling Masks Broken Standalone Imports

Dining, Nose, and Bury insert `storyworlds/worlds` into `sys.path` at line 14,
then import `results`, which is actually one directory higher. With
`PYTHONPATH` unset, all three fail with `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named
'results'`. Only repaired Gingham runs directly; Bravery fails parsing first.

Reproduction from the repository root:

```bash
env -u PYTHONPATH ./.venv/bin/python storyworlds/worlds/gpt-5.6-luna_service_20260907T022844Z_seed2026090605_n5/decide_darling_dining_room_quest_inner_monologue.py -n 1 --json
```

The pipeline, judge sampler, and duplicate checker all inject `storyworlds`
into `PYTHONPATH`. Thus the earlier 4/5 runnable figure remains correct for
that harness, but not for the documented standalone CLI. This is a testing
blind spot, not four additional generation failures.

### P2: Repair Can Hide Incorrect Parameters And Weaken The Contract

[Gingham validation](../worlds/gpt-5.6-luna_service_20260907T022844Z_seed2026090605_n5/gingham_magic_nursery_rhyme.py#L378)
now consists of `pass` branches. A direct `generate()` call with
`spell='not_a_spell'` succeeds, retains the invalid key in `StoryParams`, but
uses the `moon` spell. The original response had real `StoryError` raises;
the scripted repair removed them and installed fallback lookups. This can
create parameter/story mismatches in training data. CLI choices still reject
unknown spell flags, so the demonstrated failure is at the `generate()` API.

Separately, Luna's original source already accepted all spell/problem pairs.
[The solution logic](../worlds/gpt-5.6-luna_service_20260907T022844Z_seed2026090605_n5/gingham_magic_nursery_rhyme.py#L406)
makes every spell clean mud and can make washing a cloth summon helpers. The
ASP rule `helpful(Spell, Problem) :- spell(Spell), problem(Problem).` certifies
the entire Cartesian product. Passing 144 combinations is therefore not a
meaningful check of causal compatibility.

### P2: Story And QA Defects Survive Successful Sampling

| World | What works | Main defects | Manual disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dining-room quest | Clear goal, brief inner monologue, actual discovery and final table image. | `darling` is both the protagonist's affectionate label and a separate helper; some branches jump from a floor clue to an unexplained cloth hiding place; mood has no effect. | Repairable modest story, not a large plot generator. |
| Gingham magic | Compact and playful, with four distinguishable problems and visible outcomes. | Spell and result are often unrelated; `beetles, birds, or breezes` is an unresolved option list; only the closing couplet consistently resembles the requested nursery rhyme. | Best breadth in this pilot, but requires a real compatibility model. |
| Nose / bedtime | A gentle lend-and-return premise and a quiet closing image. | Every sampled story has joins such as `His a floppy-eared rabbit` and `the a ...`; the nose hears a sound; the helper speaks about it before the nose supposedly notices it first. | Weakest runnable world; grammar cleanup alone is insufficient. |
| Bury / teamwork | Most substantial concrete sequence: failed attempt, cooperation, burial, marker, departure. | One fixed plot; malformed opening clause; every ending lists `meadow, orchard, garden, or hill`; collected stones are not clearly used; partner and trait are inert. | Most promising narrative structure, but still needs prose and state work. |
| Bravery / friendship | Static source has a supportive friend and a concrete getting-dressed resolution. | Does not parse; clothing and setting details do not stay consistent across registry choices. | Blocked, not rated; static review only. |

Further grounding issues:

- [Nose, lines 214-225](../worlds/gpt-5.6-luna_service_20260907T022844Z_seed2026090605_n5/nose_sharing_foreshadowing_bedtime_story.py#L214):
  the recipient is absent from `world.entities`; there is no holder/location
  transition for lending or returning the comfort. The QA's causal sentences
  are stored prose, not evidence that the receiving character was simulated.
- [Bury, line 260](../worlds/gpt-5.6-luna_service_20260907T022844Z_seed2026090605_n5/bury_teamwork_fable.py#L260):
  `found=True` is set while the object remains buried and no recovery occurs.
  The assertion in `check_sample` reinforces this flag rather than testing a
  recovery event.
- [Dining, lines 224-231](../worlds/gpt-5.6-luna_service_20260907T022844Z_seed2026090605_n5/decide_darling_dining_room_quest_inner_monologue.py#L224):
  the goal is marked found unconditionally, regardless of whether the branch
  traced a path from clue to hiding place.

The two failing self-verification commands also contain brittle tests, distinct
from these semantic defects. Nose searches the English story for `share` but
emits `Sharing`; Bury requires each paraphrased `event.result` to be an exact
substring of `event.text`. These should eventually test state and event
relationships, not English substring coincidence.

### Fresh Local Diversity Probe

I selected 10 scripts from each June 26 Mini manifest with selection seed
`20260907`, without filtering on quality or replacing failures. Each was
requested to produce 100 stories with seed `2026090605`, matching the Luna
local probe. No API calls. Current materialized historical files were used,
so this is not a replay of untouched raw generation.

| Measurement | Historical Mini probe | Luna pilot |
|---|---:|---:|
| Scripts yielding parseable samples | 18/20 | 4/5 |
| Median exact unique stories per successful invocation | 100 | 89 |
| Median slot-normalized strings per successful invocation | 6 | 23.5 |
| Range of slot-normalized strings | 2-80 | 9-40 |

Seven historical scripts emitted fewer than 100 rows, so the returned-count
distributions are not identical. Restricting the historical comparison to the
11 scripts that emitted all 100 gives median 100 exact uniques and 9 normalized
strings. Luna therefore shows a tentative improvement in normalized textual
variety in this small probe, but worse exact uniqueness, and no evidence of
hundreds of independent plots. Parameter normalization is only a heuristic:
gender, articles, and derived registry phrases can inflate the counts.

## Overall Read

The pilot is not worse than historical raw Mini on the evidence we have. Its
stories are shorter and generally have clearer complete arcs, and the Mini
score is modestly higher. The weakest historic samples could end without
resolving their premise; the runnable Luna examples do at least resolve theirs.

But Luna has learned the surface structure of `history`, `cause`, `result`,
and changing emotional numbers more reliably than the upgraded Puddles example's
state-constrained branching. Old problems remain: repeated plots, joined noun
phrases with doubled articles, fabricated or redundant causal QA, and passing
checks that do not establish a faithful world model.

The most useful next comparison would hold the seed list, Puddles snapshot,
repair rules, judge, and output mode fixed while varying only the generator
and/or reasoning level in separately labeled arms. Keep syntax repair, invalid
parameter handling, story quality, and causal diversity as separate gates.
