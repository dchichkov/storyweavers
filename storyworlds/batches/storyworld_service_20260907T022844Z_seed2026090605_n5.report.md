### Manual Findings

- Diversity remains weak. Nose and Bury have one fixed event sequence, with no
  alternative causal path. Dining has three search-method branches but the same
  resolution. Mood/trait/partner parameters often do not affect the story.
- The template's event/history and cause/result structure transferred, but the
  eight causal paths in Puddles did not reliably transfer. Tracking feelings
  and setting success flags is not the same as letting state choose events.
- Nose repeatedly emits doubled articles such as `His a floppy-eared rabbit`
  and `the a small brown bear`. It also has a nose notice a sound. Its failing
  verification is a separate test bug: it requires the substring `share`,
  while the tested story uses `Sharing`.
- Bury emits the literal options `meadow, orchard, garden, or hill` in every
  ending and joins incompatible clauses in its opening. Its verification
  requires `event.result in event.text` even though those fields paraphrase
  each other. The failure does not by itself establish a simulation failure.
- Dining ambiguously uses `darling` as both the protagonist's affectionate
  label and a separate helper. Gingham freely combines spells and problems,
  allowing, for example, washing a cloth to summon helpers without explaining
  why. Its ASP gate accepts every registered spell/problem combination.
- Both original nursery-rhyme scripts had mismatched string quotes. The
  broad scripted repair rescued one but also disabled its validation raises
  and added fallback lookups. A repaired green check is therefore not evidence
  that invalid-choice handling is sound.

This is a diagnostic pilot, not evidence that Luna outperforms Mini or that the
Puddles upgrade improved generation: there is no matched control run. The batch
is cheap, but it is not ready to scale as high-diversity training data.
