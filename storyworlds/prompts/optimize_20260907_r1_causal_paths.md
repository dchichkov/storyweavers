Build a family of complete stories within the requested domain, not one source
tale with interchangeable nouns. Keep the spoken dialogue requirement.

- Implement three or four genuinely different initial problems. Give each at
  least two compatible ways to resolve it. A solution must change the actions,
  what a character learns, and the final physical scene, not just the method's
  label inside an otherwise identical paragraph.
- Let a second state variable, such as a resource limitation, mistaken belief,
  or another character's goal, change which action can work. Some paths should
  need a discovery, negotiation, failed attempt, or help that other paths do not.
  Choose only coherent combinations; do not cross every problem with every fix.
- Render the events actually executed. Dialogue should reveal the particular
  misunderstanding, disagreement, or useful information in that path. Do not
  route every path through the same conversation and explicit moral.
- Author several natural ways to open, speak, react, and close each applicable
  scene. Select them with the seeded prose RNG, preserving the same event facts.
  Vary sentence construction and optional concrete details, not arbitrary noise.
- In ordinary -n sampling, sample all meaningful parameters rather than silently
  keeping default problem, solution, voice, and mood. The prose seed must affect
  actual wording. Do not add random identifiers or irrelevant words to create
  artificial uniqueness. Every draw must remain a complete, grounded story.
- Keep names, object properties, locations, and QA tied to the chosen entities
  and executed path. A phrase written for one object or problem must not leak
  into another branch. Use a small compatible registry rather than duplicated
  giant stories or an unbounded action loop.
