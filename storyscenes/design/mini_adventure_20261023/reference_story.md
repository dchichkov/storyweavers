# The Smallest Sea

<!-- scene: 01 | A captain and a crew -->

Tom stood on the sofa with a paper hat over one eye.

"I'm the captain. You're the crew."

"What does the crew do?" Lily asked.

"What the captain says."

Lily collected the cushions.

"Wait," Tom said. "You can be the explorer."

She stopped. "An explorer chooses where to go."

<!-- scene: 02 | A real destination -->

Mom brought a folded map.

"There's treasure in the garden. I hid it before the rain."

Tom reached, but Mom waited until Lily had a corner too.

"No digging up my flowers."

"Even for treasure?"

"Especially for treasure."

An enormous red cross marked the apple tree.

<!-- scene: 03 | Reading the map -->

Tom spread the map.

"We go left."

Lily looked through the back window. "That's the fence."

"Perhaps there's a gate."

She turned the map until its little house faced the same way as theirs.

"Oh," Tom said.

He gave her the map. "You'd better carry that."

She pocketed it.

<!-- scene: 04 | The previous expedition -->

Lily found a damp paper flag behind the boots.

"From our last ship."

"That one sank," Tom said.

"You sat on it."

"It wasn't built for captains."

Lily smoothed the flag. There was still a biscuit crumb stuck to the tape. She put it on the windowsill to dry.

<!-- scene: 05 | Boots are not enough -->

They pulled on their boots. Tom buttoned his new red coat, with its brass buttons.

Mom glanced outside.

"In that coat?"

"I've got boots."

Lily crouched and pointed to the space above them.

"The mud can go higher."

Tom drew the coat's hem up. He could hardly walk like that.

<!-- scene: 06 | A different uniform -->

Mom offered his old green jacket.

"But the red one looks like a captain's."

"You can still give orders in green," Lily said. "I'll hear them."

He changed jackets, hanging the red coat on its hook.

He straightened his hat.

"Crew ready?"

"Explorer," said Lily.

"Explorer ready?"

"Now I am."

<!-- scene: 07 | Leaving port -->

They stepped into the garden. Water fell from the gutter onto Tom's hat.

"We're under attack!"

He ducked. Lily stepped sideways, out of the drip.

"The enemy can't turn."

Tom inspected his soggy hat. He folded the wet bit underneath, making the front shorter and rather crooked.

"Battle damage," he said.

<!-- scene: 08 | Entering the cave -->

They passed the summerhouse. Tom opened its door.

"A cave. We should explore it."

"The treasure's at the tree."

"There might be two treasures."

Lily followed him inside. The shutters were closed. Tom switched on the light. A deckchair waited in one corner, with a coat hanging above it.

"Someone's already here," he whispered.

<!-- scene: 09 | Making it dark -->

Tom switched off the light.

"A proper cave!"

The coat became a tall shape. Lily caught his sleeve.

"Put it back on."

"It's only the coat."

"Then I want to see the coat."

He clicked the switch. Sleeves hung above the chair.

"Sorry," he said.

Lily let go. "Leave it on."

<!-- scene: 10 | A smaller Tom -->

Lily found height marks on the doorframe.

"You used to be this tall."

Tom stood beside the lowest mark. It barely reached his shoulder.

"I don't remember being that small."

"I do. You called this the sandwich house."

"That's a better name."

They checked the cushions, just in case.

<!-- scene: 11 | Leaving the cave -->

Outside again, Tom reached back towards the light switch.

"You said leave it on."

"While I was in there."

He switched it off. Lily pulled the door shut behind them.

The loose latch bounced open.

"Lift it," Tom said.

She lifted the door by its handle. This time the latch caught.

<!-- scene: 12 | The smallest sea -->

A puddle covered the path. Beyond Mom's flowers stood the apple tree.

Tom planted his feet.

"The sea."

Lily could see the stepping stones beneath the brown water.

"A small sea."

"Still counts."

He stretched towards a stone. His boot couldn't reach without a jump. He drew it back.

<!-- scene: 13 | Testing a bridge -->

A board lay beside the path. Tom laid it across the puddle.

It almost reached.

"We could jump the last bit."

Lily pressed the board with her boot. One end slipped underwater, splashing Tom's green sleeve.

Both froze.

"Not a bridge," Tom said.

Together they pulled it back onto dry ground.

<!-- scene: 14 | Another way round -->

Tom looked at the dry earth between Mom's flowers.

Lily shook her head.

"She'll notice."

"I wasn't going to."

He had been going to.

Lily pointed to a longer path around the bed, beside the fence.

"Land all the way."

Tom stepped aside.

"Show me."

She led, and he followed without giving an order.

<!-- scene: 15 | Under the cross -->

At the tree, Lily checked the map: a flowerpot beneath the cross.

They found an upside-down pot nearby.

Tom lifted one edge. Lily reached underneath.

"Something cold."

"Gold?"

She pulled out a round biscuit tin.

Tom lowered the pot.

"It could be gold in a biscuit tin."

"It could be biscuits."

<!-- scene: 16 | Opening the treasure -->

Tom tried the lid. His wet fingers slipped.

"Hold the bottom," he said.

Lily held the tin firmly. Tom dried his hands on his jacket and tried again.

The lid came off with a pop.

Two ginger biscuits lay inside. One was broken.

"Treasure," Lily said, reaching for a piece.

<!-- scene: 17 | Dividing the spoils -->

"Captains get the whole one," Tom said.

Lily stopped reaching.

He divided the broken pieces into two piles, then broke the whole biscuit and added half to each.

"Explorers get half."

"Half of which?"

"Both."

She checked the piles, moved one small crumb, and nodded. They ate beneath the dripping apple leaves.

<!-- scene: 18 | The voyage home -->

Mom called them home.

Tom closed the tin. They took the long path home.

Beside the puddle, he paused.

"Tomorrow we could bring a longer board."

"Or keep going this way."

He considered the path.

"We haven't named this bit."

"Biscuit Passage," Lily said.

Tom nodded. That was worth putting on the map.

<!-- scene: 19 | Back on dry land -->

They left their boots on the mat. Tom put the tin on the kitchen table and hung his damp green jacket beside the clean red coat.

Mom touched the muddy splash on his sleeve.

"Rough voyage?"

"The bridge wasn't finished," he said.

Lily began to laugh. Then Tom joined her.

<!-- scene: 20 | The next captain -->

On the sofa, Lily added Biscuit Passage to the map.

Tom took off his hat and offered it to her.

"Your turn tomorrow."

She put it on. The shortened brim stayed above her eyes.

"Where are we going?" he asked.

Lily moved over, making room for him beside the map.

"Let's choose."
