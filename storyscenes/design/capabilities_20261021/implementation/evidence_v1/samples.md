# Local capability samples

## library_return

Nia needed the atlas outside, but Inez needed it returned to the cabinet by five.

Inez wanted to ask Nia to return the atlas to the return cabinet.

"Will you return the atlas to the return cabinet?" Inez asked Nia.

"Yes, I accept," Nia answered Inez's request to return the atlas to the return cabinet.

Inez offered Nia a loan of the atlas. "It must come back to the return cabinet," Inez said.

Nia understood the loan's return conditions.

"Yes," Nia answered the loan offer.

"I am ready to receive the atlas," Nia told Inez.

Nia collected the atlas. The loan had now begun.

Inez still owned the atlas; Nia was responsible for returning it.

"I promise to return the atlas to the return cabinet by time 5," Nia told Inez.

Nia put the atlas in the carrying bag.

Nia picked up the carrying bag from the reading room.

Nia went from the reading room to the courtyard.

Nia put the carrying bag in the courtyard.

Nia picked up the carrying bag from the courtyard.

Nia went from the courtyard to the reading room.

"Can you accept the atlas as my return?" Nia asked Sol.

"No," Sol answered the return offer.

"I am not authorized to settle this loan," Sol explained.

Inez authorized Nia to deposit the atlas at the return cabinet.

Nia returned the atlas to the return cabinet. The latch clicked shut.

That authorized return settled Nia's loan of the atlas.

Nia had kept the promise by time 5.

The cabinet latch was closed. Nia could set the empty bag down.

Ari inspected the tide gauge. The tide gauge was at the workshop at time 0.

Ari accepted that the tide gauge was at the workshop at time 0.

Ari sent Cy this report: "The tide gauge was at the workshop at time 0."

Bo closed the instrument case.

Bo picked up the instrument case from the workshop.

Bo went from the workshop to the jetty.

Bo put the instrument case in the jetty.

Cy received Ari's report.

Cy accepted that the tide gauge was at the workshop at time 0.

Ari went from the workshop to the jetty.

Ari opened the instrument case.

Ari inspected the tide gauge. The tide gauge was at the jetty at time 2.

Ari revised the earlier belief and accepted that the tide gauge was at the jetty at time 2.

At the jetty, Ari could finally point to the gauge. The old report still described the earlier visit, not the present one.

## observatory_strict_boundary

Vale needed the star chart for the next observing session. Noor had plans of her own.

Vale wanted to ask Noor to hand the star chart to Vale.

"Will you hand the star chart to Vale?" Vale asked Noor.

"Yes, I accept," Noor answered Vale's request to hand the star chart to Vale.

"I promise to hand the star chart to Vale before time 8," Noor told Vale.

"I am ready to receive the star chart," Vale told Noor.

At time 8, Noor handed the star chart to Vale.

Noor had not performed the promised action before time 8. The promise was breached; a later action could not make it timely.

Vale wanted to ask Noor to hand the calibration card to Vale.

"Will you hand the calibration card to Vale?" Vale asked Noor.

"No, I decline," Noor answered Vale's request to hand the calibration card to Vale.

"I want to finish my own observation before taking another job," Noor explained.

Vale held the star chart. Noor kept her calibration card for her own work.
