## bakery

## greenhouse

All ten stories share the same central premise: Mara neglected Ivo's beans, lied about the tap, and seeks to repair the friendship in the greenhouse. There are meaningful branches in the emergency response and trust outcome: s000029 and s000057 leave the beans rejected; s000047, s000073, s000068, and s000005 choose moving plants and end with restricted future access; s000034 and s000096 repair or stabilize the vent but retain supervision; s000043 and s000085 culminate in renewed access through the spare key. Much prose and scene structure is repeated nearly verbatim, so this sample shows limited overall diversity rather than evidence about the full generator.
