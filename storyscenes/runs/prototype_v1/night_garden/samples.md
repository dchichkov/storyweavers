## The Moonlit Welcome — seed 1000

The garden glittered with dew beneath the moon. Ada watched the quiet edges while Ben imagined the most impressive welcome a moth had ever seen. They soon discovered that welcoming someone was not the same as making everything brighter.

“Now it will look splendid!” Ben said, turning the lamp up. Ben lit the lamp brightly for the welcome. Its bright beam stretched a long shadow across the garden.

Ada leaned toward the edge without rushing. “There you are,” she whispered. Ada watched the edge where Mica waited. Ada learned which edge Mica was watching, instead of guessing.

Ada checked the lamp carefully. “Before we change anything, we should know where it is.” Ada checked where the lamp was standing. She tucked that useful fact away before suggesting a change.

“The shadow is telling us something,” Ada murmured, studying its shape. Ada inspected the lamp's shadow. The shadow reached farther than anyone had expected.

Ada followed the shadow with one finger. “This is the clearer way in,” she said. Ada traced the long shadow to the clearer landing edge. Her careful tracing pointed toward the flowers side.

“Mica needs a gentler edge and room to land,” Ada told the steward. Ada explained which landing edge needed gentler light. The steward now knew which landing edge Ada had discovered.

Ben turned the lamp down. “Impressive can mean comfortable, too.” Ben turned the lamp down to a quieter glow. The garden kept its welcoming glow, but the sharp brightness eased.

They moved the sign aside and opened the flower bed. “A landing place needs to be a real place,” Ben said. The children moved the sign and cleared the flower bed. Leaves and clutter no longer blocked the chosen approach.

Together they set the sign in the new circle. “This is ready,” Ben said, “but only if Mica agrees.” Ada and Ben set the flower-sign welcome circle by the open bed. The circle now offered a reachable resting place by the flowers edge.

Mica circled once, then twice. “Your turn,” Ada whispered. “Choose the place that feels right.” Mica settled safely on the flower sign in the welcome circle. Mica settled safely, her wings folding over the flower sign. Ada and Ben watched quietly, letting the changed arrangement speak for itself.
