# Harbor: one hundred independent positions

Seeds 10000-10099, same v9 generator. All 100 serialized worlds replayed;
96 exact texts and 30 mechanical plot descriptors. Both judges graded every
story together: storytelling **7.79/7.78**, diversity **5/5** (Terra/Luna).
Individual story quality holds up; many near-duplicate causal structures remain.

Manual review sampled every observed outcome, including the chart alternative
after a declined loan, two-trip delivery, shallow-pool postponement, and a
correct parcel whose shutter still needs opening. The endings now visibly use
the delivered resources or acknowledge the lost opportunity. A timely boat
return remains a return with time left, not an already-performed sailing trip.

Whole-set XZ: 131,892 raw bytes to 6,960 bytes, 5.28%, 69.6 bytes/story.
Exact uniqueness is mostly wording and small route/timing differences, not
96 meaningfully independent plots. Both judges explicitly flag that distinction.
