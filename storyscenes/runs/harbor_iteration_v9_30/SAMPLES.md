On the quay, Cy held a little wooden boat with a red sail. The boat was ready for the harbor pool; Cy was ready to see it float. First came the measuring stick: the pool had to be deep enough. And the harbor timetable allowed no new launches after the ferry bell.

Ari found the stick in Bo's tool case at the workshop and sent Cy a message. "Your stick is in the workshop. I have just seen it in the case."

The message was still on its way when Bo carried the case to the jetty. Cy's stick traveled with the tools, without any message of its own.

The message was old. Cy nearly set off, then stayed beside the timetable. "I hope you have looked again, Ari." The little red sail waited too.

No newer message arrived while Cy waited. Waiting had not found the stick. Cy went to check the workshop instead.

Cy went to the workshop. The place where the case had stood was empty. Cy read Ari's message again, then folded it away. The boat would need another plan.

There was one other place to try: the jetty. "Come on, boat," said Cy. "One more port." Cy went there to look.

At the jetty, the measuring stick lay inside the tool case. "There you are," Cy said, picking it up. Cy held it beside the boat like a very tall mast.

The closed workshop path had already taken Cy the long way around by the quay. At the pool, Cy lowered the measuring stick into the water. There was enough water under the little hull. "You will fit," Cy said.

There was still time. "A small boat deserves a proper voyage," Cy said, and put the boat on the harbor pool. After all the carrying, the red sail finally belonged over water.
