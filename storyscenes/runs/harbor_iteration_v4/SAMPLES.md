On the quay, Cy held a little wooden boat with a red sail. The boat was ready for the harbor pool; Cy was ready to see it float. First came the measuring stick: the pool had to be deep enough. And the harbor timetable allowed no new launches after the ferry bell.

At the workshop, Ari checked Bo's tool case. There was Cy's measuring stick. "Found it," Ari told Cy in a message. "In the case, here in the workshop."

At the workshop, the measuring stick lay inside the tool case. "There you are," Cy said, picking it up. Cy held it beside the boat like a very tall mast.

At the pool, Cy lowered the measuring stick into the water. Only the tip came back wet. Cy held it against the hull. "You would sit on the mud." The pool was too shallow.

Cy held the boat over the pool, then drew it back. "Not on the mud. You are a boat, not a sled." The stick had spoiled the plan, but it had saved the boat. Cy carried it back to the workshop.

The washbasin was closed. Cy set the boat on the dry shelf instead. "Not today. You would scrape your bottom." The sail was still red and the hull was still dry. Cy kept the measuring stick in hand for the next try.

Q: What did Cy want to do, and what had to happen first?
A: Cy wanted to try the wooden boat in the harbor pool before launch time ended. First, Cy had to check the water with the measuring stick.

Q: What did Ari's first message tell Cy?

Q: Where did Cy finally find the measuring stick?

Q: How did Cy check whether the pool was deep enough?
