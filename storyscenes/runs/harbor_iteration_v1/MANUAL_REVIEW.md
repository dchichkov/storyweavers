# First local harbor prototype

Ten predeclared seeds 0-9; all replay, three physical endings, ten exact texts.
No paid judge calls. This is a local checkpoint, not an accepted quality result.

All ten stories and their QA were read. They are markedly less administrative
than the original harbor demonstration, but contain repeated framing and several
explanatory asides about the difference between reports and physical objects.
Five of the ten positions happen to receive the first message immediately and
share the early handoff plot. Exact uniqueness is not ten different plots.

Caught before paid evaluation: the renderer used the global closed-path flag to
narrate Cy's detour even when only Bo/Ari took the detour. In seed 9, Cy travels
from the quay directly to the jetty. The next revision must condition that prose
on Cy's actual pre-measurement travel events, not someone else's route. A second
minor edit attributes the pool-check dialogue explicitly.

Mechanical-signature counts in this version include the global path flag and
omit Bo's opening of the case. They are provisional descriptors, not a semantic
diversity measurement. Preserve this batch; do not update its saved prose.
