Ari had drawn the harbor on one sheet of paper. Cy had made a boat small enough to sail in a tub. Both were ready to show their work; the harbor was not quite ready for them. Ari wanted the chart hanging upright, and Cy wanted water underneath the boat. Ari also wanted to leave a lantern shining at the jetty. Bo waited beside the covered launch tub. Its heavy lid needed the tools and Ari's rope to lift it.

Ari carried the chart to the jetty and fitted it into the board's clamps. "You have brought a map to the place itself," Bo said. "Now we can see whether I got it right," Ari answered. The paper stayed up without the rope.

Ari set the open lantern beside the tub at the jetty. Its light lay across the covered tub, ready for the work still to come. "A small harbor light," Bo said.

With the rope out of the case, Cy could manage the tools. The little boat came along too, still dry. Cy set the case beside Bo at the jetty. "I have brought nearly everything," Cy said. Bo looked for the coil. "Nearly is not quite a handle."

Ari brought the rope to the jetty. Bo used it with the tools to lift the heavy cover. Cy leaned toward the water. "It looks much more like a harbor with the lid off." For the first time, there was room for the boat to go somewhere.

Cy lowered the boat onto the water and opened both hands. For a moment the little hull rested still. "It does not need me to hold it," Cy said. Bo watched it float. "That was the idea."

The drawn harbor stood on the jetty board beside the real one. Below it, Cy's little boat had water instead of paper underneath it.

Q: What were Ari and Cy trying to arrange?
A: Ari wanted to hang the chart and arrange the lantern. Cy wanted to launch the little boat in the tub.

Q: Where did Ari put the chart instead of using the window?
A: Ari mounted it on the jetty board. The clamps held it without using the rope.

Q: What did Ari bring to light the launch area?
A: Ari brought the lantern with its shutter open and set it at the jetty. The bulb lit the launch area.

Q: Why could Cy carry the case once the rope was removed?
A: The tools and rope together were too heavy. Removing the rope left a load Cy could carry to the jetty.

Q: What made it possible to open the launch tub?
A: The rope and the case of tools were together at the jetty. Bo used them to lift the cover.

Q: What happened when Cy let go of the boat?
A: The boat floated on the tub water. Cy no longer had to hold it.

Q: What was arranged by the end?
A: The chart hung on the jetty board, and Cy's boat floated in the tub. The lantern stayed at the jetty.

Ari took the chart and rope to the quay window. The rope held the paper upright. "There. A harbor that cannot get your feet wet." Cy's boat was already on real water; now the paper harbor had its turn.

Ari set the open lantern beside the tub at the jetty. The light reached the water where Cy would lower the boat. "A small harbor light," Bo said.

The chart hung at the window, and the lantern stayed by the water. Cy's boat floated beside its light, smaller than the harbor Ari had drawn and quite large enough for itself.

Q: What were Ari and Cy trying to arrange?
A: Ari wanted to hang the chart and arrange the lantern. Cy wanted to launch the little boat in the tub.

Q: Why could Cy carry the case once the rope was removed?
A: The tools and rope together were too heavy. Removing the rope left a load Cy could carry to the jetty.

Q: What made it possible to open the launch tub?
A: The rope and the case of tools were together at the jetty. Bo used them to lift the cover.

Q: What happened when Cy let go of the boat?
A: The boat floated on the tub water. Cy no longer had to hold it.

Q: How did Ari first hang the chart?
A: Ari hung it from the rope in the quay window. The paper was upright instead of lying flat.

Q: What did Ari bring to light the launch area?
A: Ari brought the lantern with its shutter open and set it at the jetty. The bulb lit the launch area.

Q: What was arranged by the end?
A: The chart hung in the quay window, and Cy's boat floated in the tub. The lantern stayed at the jetty.
