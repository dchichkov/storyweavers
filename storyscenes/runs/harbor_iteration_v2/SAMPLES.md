On the quay, Cy held a little wooden boat with a red sail. The boat was ready for the harbor pool; Cy was ready to see it float. First came the measuring stick: the pool had to be deep enough. And the harbor timetable allowed no new launches after the ferry bell.

At the workshop, Ari checked Bo's tool case. There was Cy's measuring stick. "Found it," Ari told Cy in a message. "In the case, here in the workshop."

Before that message reached Cy, Bo picked up the tool case. "I need the case at the jetty," Bo said. The stick went along inside it.

Cy heard the message, then paused. Ari had seen the stick earlier. That was useful, but it was not the same as seeing it now. Cy gave another message a chance to arrive.

Ari made a fresh inspection and sent another message. "At the jetty," it said. "I have just checked." Cy could use that, without pretending a message was the stick itself.

Cy found the stick in the case at the jetty and picked it up. "There you are." The wooden boat and its measuring stick were together at last.

At the pool, Cy used the stick to check the depth. "A boat is small. A harbor is not," Cy said. The water was deep enough for the wooden boat.

The launch time had not passed. Cy set the wooden boat on the pool. "A small boat deserves a proper voyage." For once, the boat was doing its own part of the journey.

A: Yes. Cy checked the depth and put the boat on the harbor pool before launch time ended.
