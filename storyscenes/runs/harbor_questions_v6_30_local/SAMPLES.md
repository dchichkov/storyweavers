Ari wanted a parcel taken to the jetty. From the workshop, the footbridge was the short way; the lane past the sheds was the long way. In Ari's mind, the parcel was already halfway across the bridge. Cy, who would have to carry it, was still standing on the floor.

"Will you take it over? The bridge will save you a walk."
"A longer walk," Cy said. "It will still be a walk."
"A much shorter one."
Cy looked toward the crossing. "Let us make sure it goes all the way across."

Ari checked the footbridge. Its barrier was shut. The short way was still short; it simply was not a way they could use.

"The lane, then," Ari said.
"Past the sheds."
"The long way."
"The way that reaches the jetty," Cy said. Ari stopped trying to shorten it with words.

Cy took the parcel along the lane, past the sheds, and down to the jetty. There were more steps than Ari had intended. At the end of them, there was the right place to put the parcel.

The parcel rested on the jetty. Behind it, the long route had become a completed journey. The short one was still only a line Ari had wanted to take.
