Ari had drawn the harbor on one sheet of paper. Cy had made a boat small enough to sail in a tub. Both were ready to show their work; the harbor was not quite ready for them. Ari wanted the chart hanging upright, and Cy wanted water underneath the boat. Ari also wanted to leave a lantern shining at the jetty. Bo waited beside the covered launch tub. Ari considered the three jobs. "We can make room for all of them." It might mean moving things more than once, but nothing had to be given up.
