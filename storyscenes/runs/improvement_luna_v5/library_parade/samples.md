## The Library Parade and the Cart with Three Jobs — seed 1000

Nia invited the town to meet in the square, but Tuck’s cart was already packed with books promised to children at the neighborhood stop. Mayor Mallow saw a grand parade waiting to happen; Tuck saw a promise with wheels. The brass bell gave one nervous wobble as the cart rolled between them.

Nia waved from the library steps. “Everyone to the square!” Her invitation fluttered through the afternoon like a paper kite, and even the cart seemed to lean toward the meeting.

“There is a secret,” Tuck said. He revealed the cart’s folding shelves, which unfolded into a tiny room with room for books, knees, and one very surprised mayor.

Tuck handed the loaded cart to Nia with both hands, as carefully as passing a promise. Together they rolled the cart toward the parade lane, its wheels humming over the library stones. Nia steered toward the neighborhood stop, where a row of eager doorways waited.

Nia performed the book route, stopping where neighborhood children waited. Each child chose a book as the brass bell gave a friendly ding, while Mallow’s enormous banner made a roof of shade.

Books traveled farther than the parade had planned. Nia’s cart stood at the neighborhood stop, bell bright and banner broad, with children tucked beneath its shade and a new story under every arm.

## The Library Parade and the Cart with Three Jobs — seed 1001

Mayor Mallow had invited everyone to the parade lane because her empty cart was destined to become the tallest, flounciest float in town. Nia arrived with books waiting for children, while Tuck patted the cart’s clever wooden sides. “A parade can have a purpose,” Nia said. Mallow heard only the word biggest.

Nia waved from the library steps. “Everyone to the square!” Her invitation fluttered through the afternoon like a paper kite, and even the cart seemed to lean toward the meeting.

Tuck told Mallow the cart had hidden folding shelves. He pulled one seam, and a wooden shelf sprang out like a sleepy tongue. “It can carry a parade,” he said, “or open one.” Mallow’s eyes became two bright buttons.

Mallow peered into the cart and discovered exactly what it held: no books at all, only a roomy wooden floor. “Splendid,” she declared. “There is space for my enormous idea!”

Mallow tied the banner high across the cart. It snapped proudly above her head. “Now behold!” she cried, climbing aboard as if the wooden floor were a mountain peak.

Mallow performed a towering loop through the parade lane, waving from beneath the high banner. Tuck rang along behind her, and the cart’s shadow stretched across the stones like a crown.

Mallow’s towering float rolled past the cheering crowd, its banner bright as a sail. Beneath it, the cart’s shadow became a crown, and Mayor Mallow wore that shadow like the silliest royal cape.

## The Library Parade and the Cart with Three Jobs — seed 1002

At the library door, Nia guarded a loaded cart of books while Mayor Mallow pointed at the stack. “Look! My parade audience!” she cried. The children were nowhere near the parade lane, but their books were waiting. Tuck lifted one eyebrow and touched the cart’s hidden wooden seams.

Nia waved from the library steps. “Everyone to the square!” Her invitation fluttered through the afternoon like a paper kite, and even the cart seemed to lean toward the meeting.

Tuck told Mallow the cart had hidden folding shelves. He pulled one seam, and a wooden shelf sprang out like a sleepy tongue. “It can carry a parade,” he said, “or open one.” Mallow’s eyes became two bright buttons.

Mallow glanced toward the waiting stack, whose bright spines seemed to point beyond the square. Nia lifted the books out, making a bright little nest for listening.

With a click and a clack, Tuck opened the folding shelves. The cart became a cozy room on wheels, ready for stories instead of trumpets. Even Mallow lowered her voice.

Together they rolled the cart toward the parade lane, its wheels humming over the library stones.

Nia opened the reading room and called, “Come in, everyone!” Her guarded stack became a welcome. Children gathered around the shelves, and Nia’s careful smile grew wide enough to hold the whole cart.

Nia performed a story inside the traveling reading room. The parade paused, the shelves opened wide, and soon the cart’s wheels vanished beneath a soft circle of listening feet.

The parade gathered around the unfolded shelves while Nia read aloud. The cart’s wheels disappeared beneath a soft circle of listening feet, and even Mayor Mallow’s biggest banner seemed to whisper.
